## IMMUNE DYSREGULATION WITH AUTOIMMUNITY, AUTOINFLAMMATION, AND IMMUNODEFICIENCY; IDAAI

### TEXT

This entry describes immune dysregulation with autoimmunity, autoinflammation, and immunodeficiency (IDAAI). Variants in the IRAK2 gene were reported by Amali et al. (2024) in patients with severe invasive infections.

### Clinical Features

Amali et al. (2024) described 2 patients who had invasive infections one after another or repeatedly. One patient had hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis triggered by herpes simplex virus, together with tuberculosis. The other patient had Streptococcus pneumoniae bacteremia and candidemia.

### Molecular Genetics

Amali et al. (2024) identified new variants in the IRAK2 gene in 2 patients with severe invasive infections. The variants impaired IRAK2's ability to ubiquitinate the signal adaptor TRAF6 and reduced TNF-alpha production. The authors suggested that IRAK2 may play a central role in host defense against infection.
