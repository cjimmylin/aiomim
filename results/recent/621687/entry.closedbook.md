### Description

Hereditary pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma syndromes are tumor-predisposition disorders. Affected people develop neuroendocrine tumors of the adrenal medulla (pheochromocytoma) or of extraadrenal sympathetic or parasympathetic paraganglia (paraganglioma). Tumors may be multiple, may produce catecholamines, and some become metastatic. ⚠︎

### Heterogeneity

Pheochromocytoma/paraganglioma is genetically heterogeneous. Susceptibility genes include the succinate dehydrogenase complex genes (SDHD, SDHAF2, SDHC, SDHB and SDHA), as well as TMEM127, MAX, VHL, RET, NF1, FH and other genes involved in the Krebs cycle and hypoxia signaling. ✗
