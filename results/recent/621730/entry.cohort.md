### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest-28 (OZEMA28) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the CDC23 gene (603462) on chromosome 5q31.2. ⚠︎

### Description

Oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest-28 (OZEMA28) is an autosomal recessive form of female infertility. Its main feature is defective oocyte maturation. Fertilization failure and early embryonic arrest have also been seen in infertile women with biallelic variants in genes encoding anaphase-promoting complex/cyclosome (APC/C) subunits.

### Clinical Features

Fan et al. (2023) reported 3 infertile women with homozygous variants in the CDC23 gene. Their infertility was characterized by oocyte maturation defects.

Cui et al. (2025) reported 2 infertile Chinese women from 2 families (cases 1 and 2) who had compound heterozygous variants in the CDC23 gene. The study also included a third proband with variants in another APC/C gene. Across the 3 probands, the phenotypes included oocyte maturation defects, fertilization failure, and early embryonic arrest. The abstract did not say which phenotype occurred in which patient.

### Inheritance

Fan et al. (2023) and Cui et al. (2025) found biallelic CDC23 variants in affected women, consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. Fan et al. (2023) found homozygous variants. Cui et al. (2025) found compound heterozygous variants and stated that the APC/C gene mutations all showed a recessive inheritance pattern.

### Molecular Genetics

Fan et al. (2023) identified 2 homozygous missense variants in the CDC23 gene in 3 infertile women with oocyte maturation defects: c.986A-G, causing tyr329-to-cys (Y329C), and c.988C-T, causing arg330-to-cys (R330C). The abstract did not say which woman carried which variant. In vitro, the Y329C variant lowered the level of CDC23 protein. The R330C variant changed where CDC23 was located in HeLa cells and mouse oocytes.

Cui et al. (2025) performed whole-exome sequencing in infertile women from 639 unrelated Chinese families. They identified 3 probands with biallelic variants in APC/C genes. Two probands (cases 1 and 2) carried compound heterozygous missense variants in the CDC23 gene: c.1277A-G, c.833A-G, c.182C-T, and c.301C-T. The abstract did not say how these variants were paired in each case. The third proband (case 3) carried compound heterozygous variants in the ANAPC13 gene (614484), c.6C-A and c.116_126del. The authors stated that all of these APC/C mutations showed a recessive inheritance pattern. In vitro, the CDC23 variants disrupted protein expression, whereas a missense variant in ANAPC13 was linked to abnormal localization in the cell. Structural modeling suggested that the variants might change chemical bonds with other APC/C subunits.

### Animal Model

Fan et al. (2023) found that knockin mice homozygous for Cdc23 Y329C reproduced the patients' phenotype. Their oocytes had low levels of CDC23 and APC4 and accumulated securin and cyclin B1. Treatment with AZ3146 rescued the phenotype.
