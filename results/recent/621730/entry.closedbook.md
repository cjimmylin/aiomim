### Description

Oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest (OZEMA) is a group of female infertility disorders in which affected women have normal menstrual cycles and hormone profiles but repeatedly fail assisted reproduction. The failure comes from arrest at the oocyte maturation stage, failure of fertilization, or early embryonic developmental arrest. ✗
