### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest-28 (OZEMA28) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the CDC23 gene. ⚠︎

### Description

Oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest-28 (OZEMA28) is a form of female infertility. It is characterized by defects in oocyte maturation (Fan et al., 2023). Failure of fertilization and arrest of early embryonic development have also been reported (Cui et al., 2025). CDC23 is one of the core subunits of the anaphase-promoting complex or cyclosome (APC/C), which is essential for the metaphase-to-anaphase transition (Fan et al., 2023). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Fan et al. (2023) reported 3 infertile women with female infertility characterized by oocyte maturation defects.

Cui et al. (2025) studied female infertility characterized by disturbances in oocyte maturation, failure of fertilization, and cessation of early embryonic growth in 3 Chinese families. Two of the probands, case 1 and case 2, carried CDC23 variants. The abstract did not state which of these phenotypes occurred in each proband. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

Fan et al. (2023) found homozygous CDC23 variants in affected women, and Cui et al. (2025) found compound heterozygous CDC23 variants with a recessive inheritance pattern. These findings indicated that OZEMA28 is inherited in an autosomal recessive manner. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In 3 infertile women with oocyte maturation defects, Fan et al. (2023) identified 2 homozygous missense mutations in the CDC23 gene: a c.986A-G transition causing a tyr329-to-cys (Y329C) substitution, and a c.988C-T transition causing an arg330-to-cys (R330C) substitution. In vitro, the Y329C mutation decreased the level of CDC23 protein. The R330C mutation changed the localization of CDC23 in HeLa cells and in mouse oocytes.

Cui et al. (2025) performed whole-exome sequencing in women with infertility from 639 unrelated Chinese families. In 2 probands (case 1 and case 2), they identified compound heterozygous missense variants in the CDC23 gene: c.1277A-G, c.833A-G, c.182C-T, and c.301C-T. The variants affected residues conserved across species and were predicted to be deleterious by MutationTaster, SIFT, and PolyPhen-2. In structural modeling, the variants were predicted to alter chemical bonds with other APC/C subunits. In vitro experiments showed that the CDC23 variants caused dysregulated protein expression. In a third proband, the authors identified compound heterozygous variants in the APC13 gene (c.6C-A and c.116_126del). The missense APC13 variant was associated with abnormal cellular localization.

### Animal Model

Fan et al. (2023) found that female mice homozygous for the Cdc23 Y329C mutation mimicked the patients' phenotype. Their oocytes had low expression of CDC23 and APC4 and accumulated securin and cyclin B1. Treatment with AZ3146 rescued the phenotype.
