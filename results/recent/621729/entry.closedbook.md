### Description

Severe congenital neutropenia (SCN) is a genetically heterogeneous group of bone marrow failure disorders. They are defined by very low absolute neutrophil counts from early life, usually with maturation arrest of myelopoiesis at the promyelocyte/myelocyte stage in the bone marrow. Affected individuals have recurrent, often life-threatening bacterial infections starting in infancy. SCN14 is an autosomal recessive form of the disorder. ✗

### Inheritance
