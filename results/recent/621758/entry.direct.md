### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that arthrogryposis multiplex congenita-9 with immunodeficiency (AMC9) is caused by homozygous mutation in the SENP7 gene.

### Description

Arthrogryposis multiplex congenita-9 with immunodeficiency (AMC9) is an autosomal recessive disorder that is lethal in infancy or before birth. Features include congenital arthrogryposis, failure to achieve developmental milestones, early respiratory failure, neutropenia, hypogammaglobulinemia, and recurrent infections (Samra et al., 2023; Kobayashi et al., 2024). Prenatally detected anomalies have included central nervous system malformations, congenital heart disease, and renal anomalies (Saad et al., 2025). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Samra et al. (2023) reported a kindred in which 4 infants had a spectrum of findings including congenital arthrogryposis, no achievement of developmental milestones, early respiratory failure, neutropenia, and recurrent infections. All died within 4 months after birth.

Kobayashi et al. (2024) reported 4 children from 3 unrelated families who had neurodevelopmental abnormalities, dysmorphism, and immunodeficiency. Their clinical presentations were characterized by hypogammaglobulinemia and intermittent neutropenia, and all 4 patients died in infancy.

Kotecha et al. (2025) reported a consanguineous family in which multiple members had a severe, lethal form of arthrogryposis multiplex congenita.

Saad et al. (2025) gave a prenatal description of a lethal syndrome in a consanguineous Egyptian family with a history of 3 fetal deaths, all with multiple congenital anomalies. Ultrasound assessment of the current fetus showed similar anomalies, including arthrogryposis multiplex congenita, central nervous system malformations, congenital heart disease, and renal anomalies.

Pal et al. (2025) reported a patient with SENP7-related fatal arthrogryposis multiplex congenita and immunodeficiency and compared the clinical features with those of previously reported cases.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of AMC9 in the families reported by Samra et al. (2023), Kobayashi et al. (2024), Kotecha et al. (2025), and Saad et al. (2025) was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. Affected individuals carried homozygous SENP7 mutations, and the families reported by Kotecha et al. (2025) and Saad et al. (2025) were consanguineous. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In affected members of a kindred with AMC9, Samra et al. (2023) used exome sequencing and segregation studies to identify a homozygous nonsense mutation in the SENP7 gene (c.1474C-T; gln492-to-ter, Q492X). Fibroblasts from the proband showed decreased SENP7 mRNA expression. Protein expression studies showed significant protein dysregulation in total cell lysates and in the chromatin fraction. Patient fibroblasts also had reduced levels of HP1-alpha, several histones, and H3K9me3. The authors suggested that loss of SENP7 leads to reduced heterochromatin condensation and aberrant gene expression.

Kobayashi et al. (2024) identified rare homozygous loss-of-function variants in the SENP7 gene in 4 children from 3 unrelated families with AMC9. The authors proposed that deficient deSUMOylation may be a novel mechanism of primary immunodeficiency.

In a consanguineous family with multiple members affected by AMC9, Kotecha et al. (2025) identified a recessive mutation in the highly conserved splice donor site of the SENP7 gene that caused mis-splicing of the affected exons.

In a fetus from a consanguineous Egyptian family with AMC9, Saad et al. (2025) used singleton exome sequencing of fetal DNA to identify a homozygous nonsense mutation in the SENP7 gene (c.745C-T; arg249-to-ter, R249X). The premature termination codon was predicted to cause degradation of the mutant mRNA by nonsense-mediated decay.

Pal et al. (2025) identified a novel missense variant in the SENP7 gene in a patient with fatal arthrogryposis multiplex congenita and immunodeficiency.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 ARTHROGRYPOSIS MULTIPLEX CONGENITA 9, WITH IMMUNODEFICIENCY**  
SENP7, GLN492TER [c.1474C>T; p.(Gln492*)]

In 4 affected infants from a kindred with AMC9, Samra et al. (2023) identified a homozygous c.1474C-T transition in the SENP7 gene that resulted in a gln492-to-ter (Q492X) substitution. The mutation was found by exome sequencing, and segregation studies were performed. Proband fibroblasts showed decreased SENP7 mRNA expression and reduced levels of HP1-alpha, histones, and H3K9me3. All affected infants died within 4 months after birth. ⚠︎

**.0002 ARTHROGRYPOSIS MULTIPLEX CONGENITA 9, WITH IMMUNODEFICIENCY**  
SENP7, ARG249TER [c.745C>T; p.(Arg249*)]

In a fetus from a consanguineous Egyptian family with AMC9, Saad et al. (2025) identified a homozygous c.745C-T transition in the SENP7 gene that resulted in an arg249-to-ter (R249X) substitution. The mutation was found by singleton exome sequencing and was predicted to cause nonsense-mediated decay. The family had a history of 3 fetal deaths with multiple congenital anomalies.
