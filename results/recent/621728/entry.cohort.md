## DEAFNESS, AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT 55; DFNA55

### TEXT

This entry describes autosomal dominant deafness-55 (DFNA55). ✗

### Animal Model

Russell et al. (2007) made Tectb-null mice by deleting exons 1 to 4 of the Tectb gene. In these mice, Tectb protein was absent from the tectorial membrane, and the striated-sheet matrix was missing along the membrane's whole length. The tectorial membrane stayed attached to the organ of Corti. However, Hensen's stripe was absent, the marginal band was lost apically, and the apical tectorial membrane was about twice as thick as in controls.

Russell et al. (2007) found that Tectb-null mice had a low-frequency hearing loss below about 20 kHz, with reduced distortion product otoacoustic emissions in that range. At higher frequencies, neural sensitivity was close to normal, and basilar membrane and neural tuning were sharper than in wildtype mice. The authors attributed the sharper tuning to a reduced acting mass of the tectorial membrane and weaker longitudinal coupling along the cochlea.

Russell et al. (2007) concluded that Tectb is required to form the striated-sheet matrix of the tectorial membrane. Without Tectb, alpha-tectorin still formed disorganized filaments but did not form an organized striated-sheet matrix. This suggested that Tectb may cross-link filaments based on Tecta.
