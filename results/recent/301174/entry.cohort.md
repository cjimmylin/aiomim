### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that neurodevelopmental disorder with or without macrocephaly (NEDMAC) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the SMARCA1 gene (300012) on chromosome Xq25-q26.1. ⚠︎

### Description

Neurodevelopmental disorder with or without macrocephaly is an X-linked disorder characterized by intellectual disability in affected males. Additional features vary and have included macrocephaly, behavioral difficulty, learning disability, ataxia, and speech delay.

### Clinical Features

Nishimura et al. (2025) reported 2 unrelated males with X-linked intellectual disability. Patient 1 had macrocephaly, behavioral difficulty, and learning disability. Patient 2 had ataxia and speech delay. The mothers of both patients were unaffected and healthy.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of NEDMAC in the families reported by Nishimura et al. (2025) was consistent with X-linked recessive inheritance. Each affected male inherited a hemizygous SMARCA1 mutation from his unaffected, healthy mother. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In 2 unrelated males with X-linked intellectual disability, Nishimura et al. (2025) used exome sequencing to identify novel hemizygous mutations in the SMARCA1 gene. Patient 1 carried a nonsense mutation (Q599X; c.1795C-T, NM_003069.5) that led to nonsense-mediated decay. Patient 2 carried a missense mutation (R448L; c.1343G-T). Both mutations were inherited from the patients' unaffected, healthy mothers. Structural modeling suggested that the R448L variant might destabilize interactions between SMARCA1 and nucleosomal DNA, contributing to an aberrant effect of the mutant protein.
