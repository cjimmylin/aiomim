## OOCYTE/ZYGOTE/EMBRYO MATURATION ARREST 26; OZEMA26

### TEXT

This entry describes oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest-26 (OZEMA26), a form of female infertility in which oocytes, zygotes or early embryos fail to develop normally during assisted reproduction. ⚠︎

### Description

Oocyte/zygote/embryo maturation arrest (OZEMA) is a group of female infertility disorders found during in vitro fertilization (IVF) or intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) cycles. Affected women have normal menstrual cycles and hormone levels. However, their retrieved oocytes arrest before maturation, fail to fertilize, or form zygotes and early embryos that arrest or degenerate. As a result, the women have recurrent failure of assisted reproduction. ✗

### Heterogeneity

OZEMA is genetically heterogeneous. Different forms are caused by mutations in genes for zona pellucida proteins, oocyte meiotic and cytoskeletal regulators, subcortical maternal complex components, and regulators of maternal mRNA decay. This entry is one numbered form in that series. ✗
