### Description

Complex cortical dysplasia with other brain malformations (CDCBM) is a group of neurodevelopmental disorders. They are defined by malformations of cortical development, such as lissencephaly/pachygyria, polymicrogyria-like cortical dysplasia, simplified gyral pattern, or heterotopia. These occur together with other brain malformations, such as abnormalities of the basal ganglia, corpus callosum, brainstem, and cerebellum. Affected individuals usually have developmental delay, intellectual disability, and often seizures and motor abnormalities. Most CDCBM forms are caused by mutations in genes that encode tubulins or microtubule-associated motor proteins. ✗

### Heterogeneity

Complex cortical dysplasia with other brain malformations is genetically heterogeneous. Earlier forms include CDCBM1, caused by mutation in the TUBB3 gene; CDCBM2, caused by mutation in KIF5C; and CDCBM3, caused by mutation in KIF2A. CDCBM4 is caused by mutation in TUBG1 and CDCBM5 by mutation in TUBB2A. ✗
