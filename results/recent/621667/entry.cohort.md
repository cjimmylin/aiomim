### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that complex cortical dysplasia with other brain malformations-17 (CDCBM17) is caused by homozygous mutation in the DMRTA2 gene (614804) on chromosome 1p32.3.

### Description

Complex cortical dysplasia with other brain malformations-17 (CDCBM17) is an autosomal recessive disorder that begins before birth. It is characterized by a severe neurodevelopmental disorder with fronto-parietal pachygyria, agenesis of the corpus callosum, and progressive severe microcephaly (Urquhart et al., 2016). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Urquhart et al. (2016) reported a consanguineous family with 3 affected sibs. The sibs had a severe neurodevelopmental disorder that began before birth. Features were fronto-parietal pachygyria, agenesis of the corpus callosum, and progressive severe microcephaly.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of CDCBM17 in the family reported by Urquhart et al. (2016) was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. The parents were consanguineous, and the 3 affected sibs carried a homozygous DMRTA2 mutation.

### Molecular Genetics

In 3 sibs from a consanguineous family with CDCBM17, Urquhart et al. (2016) used autozygosity mapping and exome sequencing to identify a homozygous 1-bp deletion (c.1197delT) in the DMRTA2 gene. The deletion was predicted to cause a frameshift, pro400-to-leu (P400Lfs*33).

### Animal Model

Urquhart et al. (2016) stated that data from mouse and zebrafish knockout models were consistent with the DMRTA2 (DMRT5) variant causing the cortical brain phenotype. They suggested that loss of DMRTA2 function leads to a novel disorder of cortical development.
