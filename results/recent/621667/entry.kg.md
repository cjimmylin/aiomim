### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that complex cortical dysplasia with other brain malformations-17 (CDCBM17) is caused by homozygous mutation in the DMRTA2 gene (614804) on chromosome 1p32.3.

### Description

Complex cortical dysplasia with other brain malformations-17 (CDCBM17) is a severe neurodevelopmental disorder that begins before birth. Its features include fronto-parietal pachygyria, agenesis of the corpus callosum, and progressive severe microcephaly (Urquhart et al., 2016).

### Clinical Features

Urquhart et al. (2016) reported 3 affected sibs from a consanguineous family with a severe prenatal neurodevelopmental disorder. Features included fronto-parietal pachygyria, agenesis of the corpus callosum, and progressive severe microcephaly.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of CDCBM17 in the family reported by Urquhart et al. (2016) was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. The 3 affected sibs were born to consanguineous parents and were homozygous for a DMRTA2 mutation. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Urquhart et al. (2016) studied 3 affected sibs from a consanguineous family with CDCBM17. Using autozygosity mapping and exome sequencing, they identified a novel homozygous 1-bp deletion in the DMRTA2 gene (c.1197delT). The deletion was predicted to cause a frameshift (Pro400LeufsTer33). The authors stated that data from murine and zebrafish knockout models were consistent with the variant causing the cortical phenotype. They suggested that loss of DMRTA2 function leads to a novel disorder of cortical development.
