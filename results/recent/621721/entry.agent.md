### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that recurrent hydatidiform mole-6 (HYDM6) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the KASH5 gene. ⚠︎

### Description

Recurrent hydatidiform moles (RHMs) are defined by the occurrence of at least 2 hydatidiform moles (HMs) in the same patient, and some are familial (Rezaei et al., 2024). HMs are abnormal human pregnancies with impaired embryonic development and excessive trophoblastic proliferation. Saharan et al. (2026) noted the potential for progression to metastatic gestational trophoblastic neoplasia (GTN) in patients with KASH5 variants.

### Clinical Features

Rezaei et al. (2024) reported a Jordanian woman (patient 439) with 3 HMs and 2 miscarriages. The paper's report of morphologic reevaluation and genotyping of molar tissue did not include tissue from this patient.

Saharan et al. (2026) reported a woman with RHM who had recurrent GTNs. Her sister, who also had recurrent moles, died of metastatic GTN.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of HYDM6 in the patients reported by Rezaei et al. (2024) and Saharan et al. (2026) is consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. Rezaei et al. (2024) found compound heterozygous variants, and Saharan et al. (2026) found a homozygous variant. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Rezaei et al. (2024) performed whole-exome sequencing on 75 unrelated patients with at least 2 HMs who were negative for recessive mutations in NLRP7 and KHDC3L. In a Jordanian woman (patient 439) with RHM, they identified 2 heterozygous variants in the KASH5 gene: c.1555C-T, resulting in an arg519-to-ter (R519X) substitution, and c.1604T-A, resulting in a leu535-to-gln (L535Q) substitution. Sanger sequencing confirmed both variants in the proband. Only R519X was inherited from her mother, so the authors concluded that the 2 variants were most likely on different parental chromosomes. In the same study, biallelic variants in FOXL2, MAJIN, SYCP2, HFM1, and MEIOB were found in other patients. ⚠︎

Saharan et al. (2026) performed whole-exome sequencing in 2 women with RHM and validated selected candidate variants by Sanger sequencing. In 1 patient, who had recurrent GTNs, they identified a very rare homozygous variant in KASH5, arg196-to-his (R196H). In the other patient they identified a heterozygous variant in FOXL2, gly187-to-asp (G187D), which had been reported in patients with premature ovarian insufficiency.

### Pathogenesis

Rezaei et al. (2024) noted that MAJIN and KASH5 mediate the attachment of telomeres to the nuclear envelope. This attachment is critical for homologous chromosome search and synapsis. The authors proposed that defects in meiotic synapsis and recombination play a major role in the formation of androgenetic complete HMs.
