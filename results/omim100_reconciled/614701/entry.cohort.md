### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 with or without midline brain defects (CDLS4) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the RAD21 gene (606462) on chromosome 8q24.11.

### Description

Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 (CDLS4) is a cohesinopathy caused by mutation in the cohesin gene RAD21. Affected individuals have growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features that overlap those of classic Cornelia de Lange syndrome (CdLS), but cognitive impairment is generally milder than in CdLS caused by mutation in other cohesin genes (Deardorff et al., 2012; Krab et al., 2020). Some patients with heterozygous RAD21 variants have holoprosencephaly (Kruszka et al., 2019; Krab et al., 2020).

### Clinical Features

Deardorff et al. (2012) reported children with RAD21 mutations who had growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features overlapping those of individuals with CdLS. Unlike children with mutations in NIPBL, SMC1A, or SMC3, these individuals had much milder cognitive impairment than those with classic CdLS.

Minor et al. (2014) reported 2 patients with a mild CdLS-like presentation. The first patient had developmental delay, hypospadias, inguinal hernia, and dysmorphic features. His mother, from whom he inherited the mutation, had a history of melanoma and other unspecified medical problems. The second patient had developmental delay, characteristic facial features, hirsutism, and hand and foot anomalies. The first patient was more mildly affected than the second.

Boyle et al. (2017) reported a family in which the index patient (IV.16) could be classified as having mild CdLS. The mother and 2 maternal aunts of the index patient carried the same RAD21 variant, but none of them had been suspected of having CdLS or a cohesinopathy, and they did not fulfill the diagnostic criteria for CdLS. ⚠︎

Gudmundsson et al. (2019) reported a 15-month-old boy with developmental delay, distinct CdLS-like facial features, gastroesophageal reflux in early infancy, testis retention, prominent digit pads, and diaphragmatic hernia. ⚠︎

Kruszka et al. (2019) reported 4 individuals with holoprosencephaly who had variants in the non-X-linked cohesin complex genes SMC3 and RAD21.

Dorval et al. (2020) reported a boy with a mild Cornelia de Lange presentation and a RAD21 mutation, and compared his clinical features with those of previously described patients carrying RAD21 intragenic mutations. They noted that, among the CdLS genes, RAD21 is associated with a milder phenotype.

Krab et al. (2020) reported 49 individuals from 33 families with RAD21 alterations, 24 of whom were previously unpublished. In the 29 individuals with full clinical information (cohort A), the phenotype was an attenuated form of CdLS compared with that caused by NIPBL or SMC1A variants, particularly with regard to facial morphology, limb anomalies, cognition, and behavior. Features in cohort A included postnatal microcephaly in 16 of 28, postnatal short stature in 10 of 27, synophrys in 19 of 28, long and/or smooth philtrum in 26 of 29, short fifth finger in 23 of 28, cleft palate in 6 of 25, heart anomalies in 9 of 23, hearing loss in 8 of 24, and gastroesophageal reflux in 13 of 25; no major limb malformations were found (0 of 29). Short stature and microcephaly typically developed postnatally. Unusual anomalies included vertebral anomalies (clefts and hemivertebrae) and relatively frequent malformations of foregut-derived structures.

Krab et al. (2020) found that most of the RAD21 patients (16 of 29, 55%) had normal or mildly impaired cognition, and all patients aged 3 years and older used some words. Behavioral problems were present in 14 of 25, mainly anxiety, ADHD, autism spectrum disorder, and obsessive-compulsive behavior; aggression and self-injurious behavior were each seen in only 1 patient. One patient (F18) with an in-frame deletion had a classic CdLS phenotype.

Goel and Parasivam (2020) reported a patient with holoprosencephaly associated with a RAD21 loss-of-function variant.

### Inheritance

The heterozygous RAD21 mutations in CDLS4 occur de novo or are inherited in an autosomal dominant pattern. Minor et al. (2014) found that a RAD21 in-frame deletion was inherited from the patient's mother, and discussed variable clinical presentation within families and low penetrance of the mutations. Boyle et al. (2017) found a RAD21 variant in a patient with mild CdLS and in the patient's mother and 2 aunts, none of whom fulfilled diagnostic criteria for CdLS; they suggested that RAD21 variants are associated with incomplete penetrance and that such individuals may be underdiagnosed. Krab et al. (2020) found that variants were familial in 9 of 21 index cases in whom inheritance could be established.

### Cytogenetics

Wuyts et al. (2002) described a boy with multiple exostoses, hypertrichosis, mental retardation, epilepsy, and brain abnormalities who had a de novo submicroscopic interstitial deletion at chromosome 8q24. The deletion overlapped the Langer-Giedion syndrome region and included EXT1 and additional genes distal to EXT1, but probably did not include TRPS1, which lies proximal to EXT1.

McBrien et al. (2008) reported a boy with an 8q24 microdeletion characterized by oligo array CGH as involving EXT1 alone, who had a mild Langer-Giedion-like phenotype as well as prominent toe pads and fat pads on the soles of his feet. Krab et al. (2020) included a patient (F21), listed as previously reported by McBrien et al. (2008) and by Deardorff et al. (2012) (patient 2), who had a de novo 1.7-Mb deletion at 8q23.3-q24.12 encompassing the whole RAD21 gene.

Among 7 deletions in the cohort of Krab et al. (2020), 6 included other genes in addition to RAD21. Whole-gene deletions ranged from 880 kb to 2.3 Mb, and 1 patient (F22) had a 27-kb deletion removing exons 1 through 9 of RAD21.

### Heterogeneity

Krab et al. (2020) suggested that biallelic RAD21 variants can cause Mungan syndrome and that monoallelic variants can cause holoprosencephaly, with or without CdLS characteristics. Whether RAD21 variants are involved in sclerocornea and schizophrenia remained uncertain.

### Molecular Genetics

In children with features overlapping CdLS, Deardorff et al. (2012) identified heterozygous mutations in the RAD21 gene, including dominant missense mutations and loss-of-function mutations. The mutations acted at the interface between RAD21 and the cohesin proteins STAG2 and SMC1A, and they impaired the cellular DNA damage response. Krab et al. (2020) later listed patients from this report, including 2 with de novo missense mutations (P376R and C585R) and 2 with deletions encompassing the whole RAD21 gene, 1 of which was shown to be de novo. ⚠︎

In 2 patients with mild CdLS-like features, Minor et al. (2014) identified heterozygous RAD21 mutations: an in-frame deletion of exon 13, inherited from the mother, and a c.592_593dup frameshift mutation. These were the first intragenic deletion and frameshift mutations reported in RAD21 in patients with atypical CdLS. ⚠︎

In a patient with mild CdLS, Boyle et al. (2017) identified a heterozygous 1-bp deletion (c.704delG) in the RAD21 gene. The deletion was also found in the patient's mother and 2 aunts, who did not fulfill diagnostic criteria for CdLS. ⚠︎

By exome sequencing in a 15-month-old boy with features of CDLS4, Gudmundsson et al. (2019) identified a de novo heterozygous in-frame deletion in RAD21 (c.1774_1776del; Q592del). The variant was not found in either healthy parent. In silico structural modeling predicted that the deletion disrupts contact between RAD21 lys591 and SMC1A residues glu1191 and glu1192, altering the RAD21-SMC1A interface. ⚠︎

Kruszka et al. (2019) reported 4 individuals with holoprosencephaly who had variants in SMC3 or RAD21. In the same report, they described holoprosencephaly in 11 individuals with variants in the X-linked cohesin genes STAG2 and SMC1A. Krab et al. (2020) included 2 of the RAD21 patients from this report: one (P14) with a de novo frameshift mutation (c.1217_1224del) and one (P12) with a frameshift mutation (c.1550dupC) inherited from an affected father. ⚠︎

Dorval et al. (2020) identified a novel RAD21 mutation in a boy with a mild CdLS presentation. Krab et al. (2020) listed this patient (F7) as carrying a de novo frameshift mutation (c.943_946del).

Krab et al. (2020) reported 49 individuals from 33 families with RAD21 alterations, comprising 24 intragenic variants and 7 microdeletions. Thirteen of the 24 sequence variants were predicted to be truncating and were scattered throughout the gene, whereas missense variants tended to cluster at protein-binding domains. Variants were familial in 9 of 21 index cases in whom inheritance could be established.

Goel and Parasivam (2020) reported a RAD21 loss-of-function variant in a patient with holoprosencephaly.

### Genotype/Phenotype Correlations

Deardorff et al. (2012) suggested that, compared with loss-of-function mutations, dominant missense mutations in RAD21 result in more severe functional defects and cause worse structural and cognitive clinical findings.

Minor et al. (2014) noted that the patient with the in-frame exon 13 deletion was more mildly affected than the patient with the frameshift mutation.

Krab et al. (2020) observed a trend toward higher CdLS scores and more impaired growth in patients with microdeletions than in those with intragenic variants, but no difference between truncating and nontruncating variants. Striking interfamilial and intrafamilial variability, especially in cognition and behavior, was not explained by mosaicism in most families. ⚠︎

Krab et al. (2020) included a family (F30) in which 3 individuals with Mungan syndrome and chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction were homozygous for a RAD21 missense variant (A622T), inherited from both parents. Heterozygous members of this family had some clinical features of the CdLS spectrum, but further clinical data could not be retrieved, and it remained uncertain whether they had a full CdLS phenotype.

### Pathogenesis

Kruszka et al. (2019) found that shRNA knockdown of STAG2 and SMC1A in human neural stem cells changed the expression of the holoprosencephaly-associated genes ZIC2, GLI2, SMAD3, and FGFR1. By whole-mount in situ hybridization in mouse embryos, Stag2 and Smc1a were expressed in the prosencephalic neural folds during primary neurulation, consistent with a role in forebrain morphogenesis. The authors concluded that the cohesin complex is an important regulator of median forebrain development.

By protein modeling and molecular dynamics of 12 RAD21 variants, Krab et al. (2020) predicted that S345P impairs and P376R over-stabilizes the RAD21-STAG2 interaction, that C585R destabilizes the RAD21-SMC1A domain, and that Q592del and L603P displace lys605, affecting SMC1A ATPase site 1. The R65Q, F600del, and A622T variants, among others, caused no structural alteration.

### Diagnosis

Minor et al. (2014) stressed the clinical value of RAD21 mutation analysis in patients with atypical forms of CdLS.

Krab et al. (2020) found that all patients in cohort A had CdLS scores of at least 5, and that no RAD21 variant would have been missed by applying the CdLS consensus criteria for molecular testing. CdLS was suspected before testing in about 60% of index cases.

### Clinical Management

Krab et al. (2020) recommended parental testing even when parents appear unaffected, as well as periodic formal neuropsychological and behavioral screening. For a RAD21 variant of uncertain significance, they recommended testing of other CdLS genes or exome/genome sequencing.

### Animal Model

Deardorff et al. (2012) showed that the RAD21 mutations disrupted transcription in a zebrafish model.
