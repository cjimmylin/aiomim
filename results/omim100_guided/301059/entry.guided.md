### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene on chromosome Xp21.1.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 is a form of primary male infertility marked by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) studied 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF and a man from an Australian cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia. All of the men showed typical MMAF phenotypes and had primary infertility. ⚠︎

Liao et al. (2022) reported a man with MMAF.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous CFAP47 variants in affected men, which shows that SPGFX3 follows X-linked inheritance.

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) used whole-exome sequencing to study 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF. Each man was hemizygous for a missense variant in the X-linked CFAP47 gene. None of the 3 variants was found in human control population genome databases, and several bioinformatic tools predicted all 3 to be deleterious. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed clearly reduced levels of CFAP47 in spermatozoa from all 3 men. In whole-exome data from another cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia from Australia, Liu et al. (2021) found a man with a hemizygous Xp21.1 deletion that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF for mutations. In 1 of them they found 2 novel mutations in CFAP47: c.706G-A, which causes a val236-to-met (V236M) substitution, and c.1337C-T, which causes a thr446-to-met (T446M) substitution. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed that CFAP47 was greatly reduced in this man's spermatozoa. WDR87 expression was also much lower, with weak signals next to the sperm nuclei. Liao et al. (2022) also showed that CFAP47 interacted with WDR87 in the midpiece of the sperm flagellum. They concluded that CFAP47 is needed to transport WDR87 during the biogenesis of the sperm flagellum.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) found that adult male Cfap47-mutated mice were sterile. Their sperm had reduced motility and abnormal flagellar shape and movement. Intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) restored fertility in these mice.
