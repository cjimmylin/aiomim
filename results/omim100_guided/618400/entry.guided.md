### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive axonal Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2EE (CMT2EE) is caused by homozygous mutation in the MPV17 gene.

### Description

Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2EE (CMT2EE) is an autosomal recessive axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy that begins in childhood, adolescence, or adulthood. It causes distal muscle weakness and atrophy, mainly in the lower limbs, along with distal sensory loss and areflexia. Unlike other disorders caused by MPV17 mutation, the reported patients had no liver or brain involvement (Choi et al., 2015; Baumann et al., 2019). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Choi et al. (2015) reported 2 patients from 2 unrelated Korean families with axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy. Both had nonconsanguineous parents. Patient 1 was a 34-year-old woman with normal early motor milestones. She first noticed weakness of the distal lower limbs at 9 years of age and began walking with short leg braces at 12 years. At age 34 years, she had distal weakness and atrophy that was worse in the lower limbs. She had severe atrophy of the intrinsic hand, foot, and calf muscles, flexion deformities of the interphalangeal joints, ankle joint deformity, and scoliosis. Patient 2 was a 22-year-old man who first had gait and balance problems at 13 years of age and began using short leg braces at 20 years. He had distal weakness and atrophy, flat feet, and atrophy of the intrinsic hand, foot, and calf muscles. In both patients, vibration and position sense were more severely affected than pain and touch, and tendon reflexes were absent. Neither patient had growth retardation, hepatomegaly, cognitive impairment, ophthalmoplegia, corneal scarring, or hypoglycemic attacks. Liver enzymes were normal, but serum lactate was mildly elevated (2.1 and 1.9 mmol/L). On electrophysiologic studies, sensory nerve action potentials were absent. Motor conduction velocities and compound muscle action potentials (CMAPs) were reduced in the median and ulnar nerves, and peroneal and tibial CMAPs could not be elicited. Visual and brainstem auditory evoked potentials were normal, as was brain MRI. On lower leg MRI, both patients had severe atrophy and fatty replacement of the soleus muscles, with relative sparing of the tibialis posterior and lateral gastrocnemius muscles. A sural nerve biopsy from patient 1 showed almost complete loss of large and medium-sized myelinated fibers, with no evidence of demyelination or onion bulb formation. A vastus lateralis muscle biopsy from patient 2 showed a few ragged-red fibers and focal subsarcolemmal accumulation of enlarged mitochondria. ⚠︎

Baumann et al. (2019) reported 5 patients from 2 unrelated families who had juvenile- or adult-onset sensorimotor axonal neuropathy without hepatocerebral involvement.

Blakely et al. (2012) reported a 21-year-old Pakistani man, born to nonconsanguineous parents, whose phenotype was broader. Walking difficulties began at age 14 years and progressed to bilateral foot drop. He also had distal sensory loss, distal wasting and weakness, clawed toes, and absent tendon reflexes. Nerve conduction studies showed an axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy, and sural nerve biopsy showed a severe chronic axonal neuropathy. Unlike the patients described above, he also had leukoencephalopathy on cranial MRI and mild hepatomegaly with patchy steatosis and early bridging fibrosis on liver biopsy. CSF protein and lactate were increased. A muscle biopsy showed ragged-red fibers, about 20% cytochrome c oxidase (COX)-deficient fibers, and multiple mtDNA deletions. His sister had died of a progressive hepatitis at 9 years of age. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of CMT2EE in the families reported by Choi et al. (2015) was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. The affected individuals carried homozygous mutations, and a mutant allele was presumed to have been inherited from each parent. Baumann et al. (2019) also found homozygous MPV17 variants in affected individuals.

### Molecular Genetics

Choi et al. (2015) performed whole-exome sequencing in 2 unrelated Korean patients with CMT2EE. Both carried a homozygous c.122G-A transition in the MPV17 gene, resulting in an arg41-to-gln (R41Q) substitution, which was confirmed by Sanger sequencing. The mutation was absent from 300 controls and from in-house exome data, and it was present at very low frequency in the Exome Sequencing Project and ExAC databases. The R41 residue is conserved across vertebrates. Knockdown of Mpv17 in NSC34 mouse motor neuronal cells reduced cell proliferation and viability against reactive oxygen species. Expression of the R41Q mutant significantly inhibited cell proliferation. The authors suggested that both loss of normal MPV17 function and a detrimental effect of the mutant protein might affect neuronal function.

Baumann et al. (2019) found homozygous MPV17 variants in 5 patients from 2 unrelated families with CMT2EE. The affected members of the first family carried the R41Q variant. The affected members of the second family carried a novel near-splice variant, c.376-9T-G, which was shown to cause an in-frame deletion of 11 amino acids.

In a man with axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy, leukoencephalopathy, and liver involvement, Blakely et al. (2012) sequenced POLG, DGUOK, and MPV17 and found a homozygous pro98-to-leu (P98L) mutation in MPV17. This mutation affects a highly conserved residue. The nucleotide change was given as c.298C-T in the figure and c.2898C-T in the text. Parental samples were not available for testing.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 CHARCOT-MARIE-TOOTH DISEASE, AXONAL, TYPE 2EE**  
MPV17, ARG41GLN [c.122G>A; p.R41Q; p.Arg41Gln]

Choi et al. (2015) identified a homozygous c.122G-A transition in the MPV17 gene, resulting in an arg41-to-gln (R41Q) substitution, in 2 patients from 2 unrelated Korean families with CMT2EE. The mutation was found by whole-exome sequencing and confirmed by Sanger sequencing. It was absent from 300 controls and from in-house exome data, and it had very low allele frequencies in the Exome Sequencing Project (0.00008) and ExAC (0.00002471) databases. The residue is conserved across vertebrate species. In NSC34 mouse motor neuronal cells, expression of the R41Q mutant significantly inhibited cell proliferation.

Baumann et al. (2019) found the R41Q variant in homozygous state in the affected members of a family with sensorimotor axonal neuropathy without hepatocerebral involvement.
