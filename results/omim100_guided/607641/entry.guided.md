### Description

Distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-14 (HMND14) is a lower motor neuron disease that Puls et al. (2003) mapped to chromosome 2p13. They showed that it is caused by mutation in the gene encoding the largest subunit of dynactin, a protein involved in axonal transport.

### Mapping

Puls et al. (2003) found linkage of a lower motor neuron disease to a 4-Mb region on chromosome 2p13.

### Molecular Genetics

In patients with lower motor neuron disease linked to chromosome 2p13, Puls et al. (2003) screened a gene in the linked interval that encodes the largest subunit of dynactin (DCTN1). They found a single base-pair change that caused an amino acid substitution, which they predicted would distort the folding of the dynactin microtubule-binding domain. In binding assays, the mutant protein bound less to microtubules than the normal protein. Puls et al. (2003) concluded that dysfunction of dynactin-mediated transport can cause human motor neuron disease.

### Pathogenesis

Puls et al. (2003) noted that impaired axonal transport in motor neurons has been proposed as a mechanism of neuronal degeneration in motor neuron disease. They found that a mutant dynactin associated with lower motor neuron disease bound less to microtubules, which supports a role for defective dynactin-mediated transport.
