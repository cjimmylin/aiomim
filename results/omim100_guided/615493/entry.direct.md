A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 (MRT37) is caused by homozygous mutation in the ANK3 gene.

### Description

Autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 (MRT37) is a neurodevelopmental disorder. Affected individuals have moderate intellectual disability, an attention deficit-hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)-like phenotype, and behavioral problems (Iqbal et al., 2013).

### Clinical Features

Iqbal et al. (2013) studied a consanguineous family in which affected members had moderate intellectual disability, an ADHD-like phenotype, and behavioral problems.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of MRT37 in the consanguineous family reported by Iqbal et al. (2013) was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. Affected members carried a homozygous mutation. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In affected members of a consanguineous family with MRT37, Iqbal et al. (2013) identified a homozygous truncating frameshift mutation in the ANK3 gene. The mutation affected the longest isoform of the gene. Iqbal et al. (2013) stated that this was the first reported familial mutation in ANK3. A Drosophila knockdown model showed memory defects, which supported the causal role of the ANK3 mutations and the gene's role in cognitive function.

Iqbal et al. (2013) also reported a patient whose ANK3 gene was disrupted by a balanced translocation that affected all isoforms of the gene. The patient had borderline intelligence, severe ADHD, autism, and sleeping problems.

### Animal Model

Iqbal et al. (2013) found that knockdown of the ANK3 ortholog in Drosophila caused memory defects. This supported a role for the gene in cognitive function.
