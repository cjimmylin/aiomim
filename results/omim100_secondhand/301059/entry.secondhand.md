### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene on chromosome Xp21.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is a form of primary male infertility. It is marked by asthenoteratozoospermia, meaning poor sperm motility and abnormal sperm shape, together with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) studied 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF and primary infertility. They also studied an Australian man with severe asthenoteratozoospermia. All of these men showed typical MMAF phenotypes.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF and found CFAP47 mutations in 1 of them.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous CFAP47 mutations in affected men and concluded that the disorder follows an X-linked pattern of inheritance.

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) performed whole-exome sequencing in 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF. Each man carried a hemizygous missense mutation in the X-linked CFAP47 gene. None of the 3 variants appeared in human control population genome databases, and several bioinformatic tools predicted them to be deleterious. CFAP47 is highly expressed in testis. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed clearly reduced levels of CFAP47 in sperm from all 3 men. Liu et al. (2021) also analyzed whole-exome sequencing data from a separate Australian cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia. In 1 man they found a hemizygous Xp21.1 deletion that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF and found 2 novel CFAP47 mutations in 1 man: c.706G-A (val236 to met; V236M) and c.1337C-T (thr446 to met; T446M). Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed that CFAP47 was greatly reduced in this man's sperm. Levels of WDR87, a protein that interacts with CFAP47, were also much lower. The weak WDR87 signals that remained were found next to the sperm nuclei.

### Pathogenesis

Liao et al. (2022) showed that CFAP47 interacts with WDR87 in the middle piece of the sperm flagellum. In sperm from a man with CFAP47 mutations, WDR87 levels were reduced and the protein was mislocalized. From these findings the authors proposed that CFAP47 is needed to transport WDR87 while the sperm flagellum is being built. They also proposed that the 2 proteins form a complex that takes part in sperm tail assembly.

### Clinical Management

Liu et al. (2021) reported that men with asthenoteratozoospermia caused by CFAP47 mutations had good clinical outcomes with intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI). In Cfap47-mutant mice, ICSI restored fertility. The authors suggested that the prognosis with ICSI is good. ⚠︎

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) produced a Cfap47-mutant mouse model carrying a frameshift mutation. Adult male mice were sterile. Their sperm had reduced motility, abnormal flagellar shape, and abnormal flagellar movement. ICSI restored fertility in these mice. ⚠︎
