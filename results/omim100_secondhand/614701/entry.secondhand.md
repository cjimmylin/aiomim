### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 with or without midline brain defects (CDLS4) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the RAD21 gene on chromosome 8q24.11.

### Description

Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 (CDLS4) is a cohesinopathy. Affected individuals have growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features that overlap those of Cornelia de Lange syndrome (CdLS). Cognitive impairment is much milder than in classic CdLS caused by mutations in NIPBL, SMC1A, or SMC3 (Deardorff et al., 2012). Compared with CdLS caused by NIPBL or SMC1A variants, the phenotype is attenuated for facial morphology, limb anomalies, and especially cognition and behavior. Some patients have holoprosencephaly, with or without CdLS features. The disorder varies markedly between and within families (Krab et al., 2020).

### Clinical Features

Deardorff et al. (2012) reported children with RAD21 mutations who had growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features that overlapped those of CdLS. Their cognitive impairment was much milder than that of individuals with classic CdLS.

Minor et al. (2014) reported 2 patients with atypical CdLS. The first had developmental delay, hypospadias, inguinal hernia, and dysmorphic features. The second had developmental delay, characteristic facial features, hirsutism, and hand and foot anomalies. The first patient was more mildly affected than the second. The first patient's mother, who carried the same mutation, had a history of melanoma and other unspecified medical problems. The authors discussed variable clinical presentation within families and low penetrance. ⚠︎

Boyle et al. (2017) reported a family in which the index patient (IV.16) could be classified as having mild CdLS. The patient's mother and 2 maternal aunts carried the same RAD21 variant but did not fulfill the diagnostic criteria for CdLS. None of these relatives had been suspected of having CdLS or a cohesinopathy before the study. ⚠︎

Gudmundsson et al. (2019) reported a 15-month-old boy with developmental delay and distinct CdLS-like facial features. He also had gastrointestinal reflux in early infancy, testis retention, prominent digit pads, and diaphragmatic hernia.

Kruszka et al. (2019) reported holoprosencephaly in individuals with variants in cohesin complex genes. These included 4 individuals with variants in the non-X-linked cohesin genes SMC3 and RAD21.

Dorval et al. (2020) reported a boy with a mild Cornelia de Lange presentation and a RAD21 mutation. They compared his clinical features with those of previously described patients who carried other intragenic RAD21 mutations.

Goel and Parasivam (2020) reported another case of holoprosencephaly associated with a RAD21 loss-of-function variant.

Krab et al. (2020) studied 49 individuals from 33 families with RAD21 alterations, including 24 previously unpublished cases. Patients came from Australia, Belgium, Canada, Denmark, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Full clinical data were available for 29 individuals from 22 families (cohort A), whose ages ranged from 0 to 61 years (median 9 years). All patients in cohort A had CdLS scores of at least 5. CdLS had been suspected before testing in about 60% of index cases. Compared with NIPBL and SMC1A cohorts, Krab et al. (2020) found that CdLS features in cohort A were less prevalent and typically less severe. Frequent features were: - long and/or smooth philtrum (90%) - short nose (88%) - concave nasal ridge (83%) - thick eyebrows (83%) - short fifth finger (82%) - long eyelashes (81%) - thin upper lip vermilion (79%) - upturned nasal tip (70%) - synophrys (68%) - arched eyebrows (67%) - low anterior/posterior hairline (61%) - postnatal head circumference less than -2 SD (57%) - clinodactyly of the fifth finger (54%) - low-set and/or malformed ears (54%) - gastroesophageal reflux (52%) - dislocated elbow or abnormal elbow extension (46%) - abnormal palmar crease (43%) - ptosis (42%) - heart anomalies (39%) - hirsutism (38%) - postnatal height less than -2 SD (37%) - micrognathia (35%) - hearing loss (33%) - cleft palate or submucous cleft palate (24%) No patient had a major limb malformation, and hands and feet were generally of normal size. Minor anomalies of the hands and feet were common, including fetal pads, abnormal flexion crease patterns, and camptodactyly. Growth at birth was generally less impaired than in the other cohorts, and short stature and microcephaly developed postnatally. Seizures occurred in 9%, and none of 24 patients had visual impairment. Krab et al. (2020) also noted some unusual anomalies: - vertebral anomalies (clefts and hemivertebrae) - malformations of structures derived from the embryonic foregut - occasional diaphragmatic hernia, anal atresia, or choanal atresia

In cohort A, cognition and behavior were also less affected than in the NIPBL and SMC1A groups (Krab et al., 2020). Sixteen of the 29 patients (55%) had normal or mildly impaired cognitive function. No patient had severe or profound disability. All patients aged 3 years and older used some words, and language problems were more prominent than motor problems. Fourteen of 25 patients (56%) had behavioral problems, mainly anxiety, ADHD, autism spectrum disorder, and obsessive-compulsive behavior. Aggression and self-injurious behavior were uncommon. Within families, variation in cognition and behavior could be considerable: some families included both patients with intellectual disability and patients with apparently normal cognition. Some carriers were diagnosed only after more severely affected offspring were recognized. Among the 20 individuals with limited clinical data (cohort B), additional phenotypes included Mungan syndrome (in patients with biallelic variants) and holoprosencephaly, with or without CdLS characteristics. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

The heterozygous RAD21 mutations in CDLS4 occur de novo or are inherited in an autosomal dominant pattern. Minor et al. (2014) reported the first familial case, in which an in-frame deletion was inherited from a mother with a mild phenotype. Boyle et al. (2017) found a RAD21 variant in a mildly affected index patient, her mother, and 2 aunts, and suggested that RAD21 variants show incomplete penetrance. In the series of Krab et al. (2020), 9 of 21 index cases with known inheritance were familial. The authors noted marked intrafamilial and interfamilial variability. ⚠︎

### Cytogenetics

Wuyts et al. (2002) reported a boy with multiple exostoses, hypertrichosis, mental retardation, and epilepsy who had a de novo deletion on chromosome 8q24. The deletion overlapped the Langer-Giedion syndrome region and involved the EXT1 gene and additional genes distal to it. It probably did not include the TRPS1 gene, which lies proximal to EXT1. Krab et al. (2020) listed this deletion among microdeletions involving RAD21 that also include neighboring genes, a contiguous gene situation that complicates attributing the phenotype to RAD21.

McBrien et al. (2008) reported a boy whose small 8q24 microdeletion, characterized by oligo array CGH, involved EXT1 but not TRPS1. He had a mild Langer-Giedion-like phenotype, which suggested a functional disturbance of TRPS1. He also had unusual features, including prominent toe pads and fat pads on the soles of the feet similar to those described in Pierpont syndrome. Krab et al. (2020) listed the same patient, as patient 2 of Deardorff et al. (2012), with a de novo 1.7-Mb deletion that included the whole RAD21 gene (8q23.3q24.12, hg19 117,571,728-119,260,904).

### Molecular Genetics

Deardorff et al. (2012) found mutations in the cohesin structural gene RAD21 in children with a CdLS-like phenotype. The mutations acted at the interface between RAD21 and the other cohesin proteins STAG2 and SMC1A. They impaired the cellular DNA damage response and disrupted transcription in a zebrafish model. The authors suggested that dominant missense mutations cause more severe functional defects, and worse structural and cognitive findings, than loss-of-function mutations. Their patients included individuals with the de novo missense mutations P376R and C585R and individuals with whole-gene deletions of RAD21 (Krab et al., 2020). In a cell line from a patient with classic CdLS, haploinsufficiency for RAD21 led to approximately halved RAD21 RNA levels (Krab et al., 2020).

Minor et al. (2014) identified 2 RAD21 mutations in 2 patients with atypical CdLS. These were the first intragenic deletion and the first frameshift mutation reported in RAD21. One patient had an in-frame deletion of exon 13, inherited from his mother. The other patient had a frameshift mutation (c.592_593dup). ⚠︎

In a patient with mild CdLS, Boyle et al. (2017) identified a heterozygous 1-bp deletion in the RAD21 gene (c.704delG), predicted to cause a premature stop codon. The deletion was also found in the patient's mother and 2 aunts. ⚠︎

In a 15-month-old boy with CdLS4, Gudmundsson et al. (2019) used exome sequencing to identify a de novo in-frame deletion in the RAD21 gene (c.1774_1776del, p.Gln592del). His 2 parents were healthy. In silico structural modeling predicted that the deletion breaks contact between RAD21 lys591 and SMC1A residues glu1191 and glu1192, altering the RAD21-SMC1A interface.

Kruszka et al. (2019) reported holoprosencephaly associated with variants in cohesin complex genes. Among them were 4 individuals with variants in SMC3 and RAD21. One of these patients carried a de novo frameshift deletion in RAD21 (c.1217_1224del) (Krab et al., 2020).

In a boy with mild CdLS, Dorval et al. (2020) identified a RAD21 mutation, which Krab et al. (2020) listed as a de novo frameshift deletion (c.943_946del).

Krab et al. (2020) described 31 different RAD21 alterations in 33 families: 7 unique deletions and 24 intragenic sequence variants. Two sequence variants, C585R and R586X, were each found in 2 families. Of the 24 sequence variants, 13 were predicted to be truncating (2 nonsense, 2 splice site, and 9 frameshift variants), and these were scattered throughout the gene. Missense variants tended to cluster in the protein-binding domains. Six of the 7 deletions included other genes in addition to RAD21. Protein modeling and molecular dynamics studies predicted that at least 6 of 12 modeled variants are likely pathogenic, through effects on the RAD21-STAG domain or the RAD21-SMC1A domain.

### Genotype/Phenotype Correlations

Deardorff et al. (2012) suggested that dominant missense mutations in RAD21 cause more severe functional defects and worse structural and cognitive findings than loss-of-function mutations. In contrast, Krab et al. (2020) found no difference in CdLS scores or growth parameters between patients with truncating and non-truncating sequence variants. They also noted that patients with haploinsufficiency due to microdeletions or truncating variants did not differ markedly from those with missense variants. Patients with microdeletions tended to have higher CdLS scores and more often had impaired growth than those with intragenic variants. However, the 2 groups did not differ in the frequency of major malformations or of cognitive and behavioral problems.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, PRO376ARG [c.1127C>G; p.Pro376Arg]

In a patient (P5) with a CdLS-like phenotype (CDLS4), Deardorff et al. (2012) identified a de novo heterozygous c.1127C-G transversion in exon 9 of the RAD21 gene, resulting in a pro376-to-arg (P376R) substitution (Krab et al., 2020). Krab et al. (2020) gave this patient a CdLS score of at least 10. Molecular dynamics modeling predicted that the substitution forms a new salt bridge between RAD21 and STAG2, over-stabilizing the interaction between the 2 proteins.

**.0002 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, CYS585ARG [c.1753T>C; p.Cys585Arg]

In a patient (P6) with CDLS4, Deardorff et al. (2012) identified a de novo heterozygous c.1753T-C transition in exon 14 of the RAD21 gene, resulting in a cys585-to-arg (C585R) substitution (Krab et al., 2020).

Krab et al. (2020) found the C585R mutation in a second family with 2 affected members. Modeling predicted that the substitution destabilizes the RAD21-SMC1A domain: arg586 loses its interaction with glu577, and the new arg585 interacts with glu583.

**.0003 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, 665-BP DEL [c.1621-388_1704+193del; p.Asp541_Gln568del]

In a patient with atypical CdLS (CDLS4), Minor et al. (2014) identified a heterozygous in-frame deletion of exon 13 of the RAD21 gene. The patient had developmental delay, hypospadias, inguinal hernia, and dysmorphic features. The deletion was inherited from his mother, who had a history of melanoma and other unspecified medical problems. Krab et al. (2020) described this deletion as a 665-bp deletion (c.1621-388_1704+193del) that removes amino acids asp541 to gln568 (p.Asp541_Gln568del). They gave the patient a CdLS score of 8 and the mother a score of at least 5. ⚠︎

**.0004 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, 2-BP DUP, 592AG [c.592_593dupAG; p.Ser198Argfs*6]

In a patient with atypical CdLS (CDLS4), Minor et al. (2014) identified a heterozygous frameshift mutation in the RAD21 gene (c.592_593dup). The patient had developmental delay, characteristic facial features, hirsutism, and hand and foot anomalies. Krab et al. (2020) described the mutation as c.592_593dupAG in exon 6, predicted to cause a frameshift (Ser198ArgfsTer6), and gave the patient a CdLS score of 12. ⚠︎

**.0005 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, 1-BP DEL, 704G [c.704delG; p.Ser235Ilefs*19]

In a patient (IV.16) with mild CdLS (CDLS4), Boyle et al. (2017) identified a heterozygous 1-bp deletion in the RAD21 gene (c.704delG), predicted to cause a frameshift and premature stop codon (Ser235IlefsTer19). The deletion was also found in the patient's mother and 2 aunts, who did not fulfill the diagnostic criteria for CdLS. Krab et al. (2020) placed the deletion in exon 7. They reported an additional affected family member carrying it and gave the family members CdLS scores of 9 to 12. ⚠︎

**.0006 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, 3-BP DEL, NT1774 [c.1774_1776del; p.Gln592del]

In a 15-month-old boy with CDLS4, Gudmundsson et al. (2019) used exome sequencing to identify a de novo heterozygous 3-bp deletion in the RAD21 gene (c.1774_1776del), resulting in deletion of gln592 (Gln592del). The boy had developmental delay, CdLS-like facial features, reflux, testis retention, prominent digit pads, and diaphragmatic hernia. In silico structural modeling predicted disturbance of the RAD21-SMC1A interface. Molecular dynamics modeling by Krab et al. (2020) predicted that the deletion moves lys605 away from ATPase site 1 of the SMC1A/SMC3 heads. ⚠︎
