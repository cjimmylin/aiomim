### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that orofaciodigital syndrome XX (OFD20) is caused by biallelic (homozygous or compound heterozygous) mutation in the RAB34 gene. ⚠︎

### Description

Orofaciodigital syndrome XX (OFD20) is a ciliopathy marked by defects in how the face and oral cavity develop, together with digit anomalies. Cardiac, cerebral, skeletal and anorectal defects can also occur (Bruel et al., 2023). Reported features include micrognathia, cleft lip and palate, short extremities, and both preaxial and postaxial polydactyly (Batkovskyte et al., 2024). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Bruel et al. (2023) reported 4 individuals from 3 unrelated families with a new form of oral-facial-digital syndrome. Along with oral, facial and digital anomalies, the patients had cardiac, cerebral, skeletal and anorectal defects. ⚠︎

Batkovskyte et al. (2024) reported a fetus whose skeletal ciliopathy phenotype was atypical. The fetus had multiple malformations: posterior neck edema, micrognathia, low-set and small ears, auricular hypoplasia, cleft lip and palate, and short extremities. It also had both preaxial and postaxial polydactyly, a combination that occurs rarely. An unaffected sib was also reported.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of OFD20 is consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. Bruel et al. (2023) found biallelic RAB34 mutations in affected individuals. Batkovskyte et al. (2024) found compound heterozygous mutations in an affected fetus, with 1 variant inherited from each parent. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Using exome sequencing, Bruel et al. (2023) identified biallelic missense mutations in the RAB34 gene in 4 individuals with OFD20 from 3 unrelated families. The mutations clustered near the C terminus of RAB34. In functional studies, the mutant proteins showed a strong loss of function. Some mutant proteins could still be recruited to the mother centriole, but cells expressing mutant RAB34 had a significant defect in cilium assembly. Bruel et al. (2023) noted that RAB34 acts selectively in cell types that use the intracellular ciliogenesis pathway.

Using genome sequencing, Batkovskyte et al. (2024) identified compound heterozygous mutations in the RAB34 gene in a fetus with OFD20. The maternally inherited mutation was c.254T-C (ile85-to-thr, I85T) and the paternally inherited mutation was c.691C-T (arg231-to-ter, R231X). An unaffected sib carried only the paternal variant. Batkovskyte et al. (2024) noted that Rab34-null mice have previously been reported with a ciliopathy phenotype that includes cleft palate and polydactyly. They considered this consistent with the malformations seen in the fetus.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

RAB34, ILE85THR [c.254T>C; p.Ile85Thr]

Batkovskyte et al. (2024) identified compound heterozygous mutations in the RAB34 gene, found by genome sequencing, in a fetus with orofaciodigital syndrome XX (OFD20). One was a c.254T-C transition, resulting in an ile85-to-thr (I85T) substitution, inherited from the mother. The other was a c.691C-T transition, resulting in an arg231-to-ter (R231X) substitution, inherited from the father. An unaffected sib carried only the paternal variant.
