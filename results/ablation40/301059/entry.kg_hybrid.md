### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene (301057) on chromosome Xp21.1.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is a form of primary male infertility characterized by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) reported 3 unrelated Chinese men with primary infertility and asthenoteratozoospermia characterized by MMAF. An Australian man with severe asthenoteratozoospermia, identified in a second cohort, also showed typical MMAF phenotypes. ⚠︎

Liao et al. (2022) reported a man with MMAF, identified among 7 men with MMAF who were screened for gene mutations.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene in affected men. They concluded that SPGFX3 follows X-linked inheritance.

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) performed whole-exome sequencing in 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF and identified hemizygous missense variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene. None of the 3 variants was found in human control population genome databases, and multiple bioinformatic tools predicted them to be deleterious. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence assays showed clearly reduced levels of CFAP47 in spermatozoa from all 3 men. In whole-exome sequencing data from an additional cohort of Australian men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia, Liu et al. (2021) identified a hemizygous deletion at Xp21.1 that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF for gene mutations. In 1 man they identified 2 novel mutations in the CFAP47 gene: c.706G-A (V236M) and c.1337C-T (T446M). Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed that CFAP47 was dramatically reduced in the man's spermatozoa. Expression of WDR87, which interacts with CFAP47, was also significantly decreased, and only weak signals were detected adjacent to the sperm nuclei.

### Pathogenesis

Liao et al. (2022) found that WDR87 was highly expressed in human and mouse testis and localized to the middle piece of the sperm tail, where it interacted with CFAP47. In spermatozoa from a man with CFAP47 mutations, WDR87 was decreased and was detected only weakly adjacent to the sperm nuclei. Liao et al. (2022) concluded that CFAP47 is necessary for the transport of WDR87 during sperm flagellum biogenesis, and that the 2 proteins form a complex involved in sperm tail assembly.

### Clinical Management

On the basis of their findings in humans and mice, Liu et al. (2021) suggested that men with SPGFX3 have a good prognosis with intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI).

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) generated a Cfap47-mutated mouse model. Adult male mice were sterile and showed reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar morphology and movement. Their fertility could be rescued by ICSI.
