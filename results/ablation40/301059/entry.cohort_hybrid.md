### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene (301057) on chromosome Xp21.1.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is a form of primary male infertility. It is characterized by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) reported 3 unrelated Chinese men with primary infertility and asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the flagella (MMAF). They also reported a man from an Australian cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia. All of these men had typical MMAF phenotypes. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed clearly reduced levels of CFAP47 in spermatozoa from the 3 Chinese men.

Liao et al. (2022) reported a man with MMAF whose spermatozoa had greatly reduced CFAP47 protein on immunoblotting and immunofluorescence. WDR87 expression in his spermatozoa was also markedly decreased, with weak signals next to the sperm nuclei.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene in affected men. They concluded that hemizygous CFAP47 mutations cause X-linked MMAF and asthenoteratozoospermia.

### Heterogeneity

Liu et al. (2021) noted that several genes previously associated with MMAF in humans show autosomal recessive inheritance, and that the cause is unknown in about 40% of affected individuals.

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) performed whole-exome sequencing in 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF and identified a hemizygous missense variant in the CFAP47 gene in each of them. The 3 variants were absent from human control population genome databases, and several bioinformatic tools predicted them to be deleterious. CFAP47 levels were clearly reduced in spermatozoa from all 3 men. In whole-exome sequencing data from a separate Australian cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia, Liu et al. (2021) identified a hemizygous Xp21.1 deletion that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF for mutations. In 1 of them they found 2 novel variants in the CFAP47 gene: V236M and T446M. CFAP47 was greatly reduced in this man's spermatozoa.

### Pathogenesis

Liao et al. (2022) found that CFAP47 interacted with WDR87 in the midpiece of the sperm flagellum. In spermatozoa from a man with CFAP47 mutations, WDR87 expression was markedly reduced, with weak signals next to the sperm nuclei. The authors concluded that CFAP47 is needed to transport WDR87 during flagellar biogenesis, and that the 2 proteins form a complex involved in sperm tail assembly.

### Clinical Management

Based on findings in Cfap47-mutant mice, in which intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) restored fertility, Liu et al. (2021) suggested a good prognosis with ICSI for men with CFAP47-associated MMAF.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) generated a Cfap47-mutant mouse model. Adult mutant males were sterile and had reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar morphology and movement. Their fertility could be rescued by ICSI.
