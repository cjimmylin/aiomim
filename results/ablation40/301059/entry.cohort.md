### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene (301057) on chromosome Xp21.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 is a form of primary male infertility marked by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF). Liu et al. (2021) reported reduced levels of the cilia- and flagella-associated protein CFAP47 in spermatozoa from affected men.

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) reported 3 unrelated Chinese men with primary infertility, asthenoteratozoospermia, and MMAF. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed obviously reduced levels of CFAP47 in spermatozoa from all 3 men. The authors also studied a cohort of severely asthenoteratozoospermic men from Australia. All men with hemizygous CFAP47 variants had typical MMAF phenotypes.

Liao et al. (2022) studied 7 men with MMAF and found CFAP47 mutations in 1 of them. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed that CFAP47 was dramatically reduced in this man's spermatozoa.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous CFAP47 variants in affected men, consistent with X-linked recessive inheritance. They concluded that hemizygous mutations in CFAP47 can cause X-linked MMAF and asthenoteratozoospermia. They contrasted this with the MMAF genes identified previously, which showed autosomal recessive inheritance. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) identified hemizygous missense mutations in the X-linked CFAP47 gene in 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF. The 3 variants were absent from human control population genome databases, and multiple bioinformatic tools predicted them to be deleterious. CFAP47 levels were obviously reduced in spermatozoa from all 3 men.

Liu et al. (2021) also used whole-exome sequencing data from a cohort of severely asthenoteratozoospermic men from Australia. In this cohort they identified a hemizygous deletion at Xp21.1 that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF for gene mutations. In 1 man they found 2 novel missense mutations in the CFAP47 gene: c.706G-A, which causes a val236-to-met (V236M) substitution, and c.1337C-T, which causes a thr446-to-met (T446M) substitution. CFAP47 was dramatically reduced in this man's spermatozoa.

### Pathogenesis

Liao et al. (2022) found that CFAP47 interacted with WDR87 in the flagellar midpiece of mouse spermatozoa. WDR87 was highly expressed in human and mouse testis and localized to the middle piece of the sperm tail. In spermatozoa from an MMAF patient with CFAP47 mutations, WDR87 expression was significantly decreased, with weak signals next to the sperm nuclei. The authors suggested that CFAP47 is needed to transport WDR87 during sperm flagella biogenesis. ⚠︎

### Clinical Management

Because ICSI rescued fertility in their Cfap47-mutant mouse model, Liu et al. (2021) suggested that CFAP47-associated asthenoteratozoospermia has a good prognosis with ICSI.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) generated a Cfap47-mutant mouse model. Adult male mice were sterile and had reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar morphology and movement. Intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) rescued their fertility.
