### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene on chromosome Xp21.1.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is a form of primary male infertility. It is characterized by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) studied 3 unrelated Chinese men with asthenoteratozoospermia characterized by MMAF, and an Australian man from a cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia. All of the men displayed typical MMAF phenotypes and had primary infertility. ⚠︎

Liao et al. (2022) reported a man with MMAF. Immunofluorescence showed that WDR87 expression was significantly decreased in his spermatozoa. Weak WDR87 signals were seen next to the sperm nuclei.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene in men with MMAF. This indicates X-linked inheritance of SPGFX3.

### Molecular Genetics

In 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF, Liu et al. (2021) used whole-exome sequencing to identify hemizygous missense mutations in the X-linked CFAP47 gene. The variants were absent from human control population genome databases and were predicted to be deleterious by several bioinformatic tools. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed clearly reduced CFAP47 levels in spermatozoa from all 3 men. In whole-exome sequencing data from a further cohort of Australian men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia, Liu et al. (2021) identified a hemizygous deletion at Xp21.1 that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF for gene mutations. In 1 of them, they identified 2 novel mutations in the CFAP47 gene: c.706G-A, resulting in a val236-to-met (V236M) substitution, and c.1337C-T, resulting in a thr446-to-met (T446M) substitution. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed that CFAP47 was dramatically reduced in the patient's spermatozoa. Liao et al. (2022) also showed that CFAP47 interacted with WDR87 in the midpiece of the sperm flagellum. WDR87 expression was significantly decreased in the patient's spermatozoa, which suggested to the authors that CFAP47 is needed for WDR87 transport during flagellar biogenesis.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) generated a Cfap47-mutated mouse model. Adult male mice were sterile and showed reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar morphology and movement. Fertility in these mice could be rescued by intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI). Based on this, the authors suggested that ICSI has a good prognosis in this disorder.
