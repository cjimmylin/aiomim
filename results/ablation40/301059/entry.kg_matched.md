### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene (301057) on chromosome Xp21.1.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 is a form of primary male infertility caused by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021). The disorder results from hemizygous mutation in CFAP47, which encodes a cilia- and flagella-associated protein that is highly expressed in testis (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) reported 3 unrelated Chinese men with primary infertility and asthenoteratozoospermia characterized by multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF). An Australian man with severe asthenoteratozoospermia from a second cohort was also found to carry a hemizygous CFAP47 variant. All 4 men carrying hemizygous CFAP47 variants showed typical MMAF phenotypes.

Liao et al. (2022) studied a man with MMAF, identified among 7 men with MMAF who were screened for gene mutations.

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) found hemizygous variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene in affected men and concluded that hemizygous CFAP47 mutations can cause X-linked MMAF and asthenoteratozoospermia, consistent with X-linked recessive inheritance. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Using whole-exome sequencing, Liu et al. (2021) identified hemizygous missense variants in the CFAP47 gene in 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF. None of the 3 variants appeared in human control population genome databases, and several bioinformatic tools predicted them to be deleterious. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed clearly reduced CFAP47 levels in spermatozoa from all 3 men.

In whole-exome sequencing data from a second cohort of Australian men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia, Liu et al. (2021) identified a man with a hemizygous deletion at Xp21.1 that removed the entire CFAP47 gene.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF for gene mutations and in 1 man identified 2 novel CFAP47 mutations: val236-to-met (V236M) and thr446-to-met (T446M). Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence of spermatozoa from this man showed that CFAP47 was dramatically reduced, and that WDR87 was also significantly decreased, with weak signals near the sperm nuclei.

### Pathogenesis

Liao et al. (2022) found that WDR87 is highly expressed in human and mouse testis and localizes to the middle piece of the sperm tail, where it interacts with CFAP47. They suggested that the 2 proteins form a complex involved in sperm tail assembly. Based on the reduced WDR87 signal in spermatozoa from a man with CFAP47 mutations, they concluded that CFAP47 is needed to transport WDR87 during sperm flagellum formation, and suggested that WDR87 may be associated with MMAF.

### Clinical Management

On the basis of their human and mouse findings, including rescue of fertility in Cfap47-mutant male mice by intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI), Liu et al. (2021) suggested a good prognosis with ICSI for men with hemizygous CFAP47 mutations.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) generated a Cfap47-mutated mouse model. Adult male mutant mice were sterile and had reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar shape and movement. Their fertility could be rescued by ICSI.
