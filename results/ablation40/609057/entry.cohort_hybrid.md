### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that epidermolysis bullosa simplex-7 with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is caused by homozygous mutation in the CD151 gene (602243) on chromosome 11p15.5.

### Description

EBS7 is an autosomal recessive disorder characterized by hereditary nephritis progressing to end-stage kidney disease, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa, and sensorineural deafness. Affected individuals are negative for the MER2 blood group antigen, which is carried by CD151 (Karamatic Crew et al., 2004). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Kagan et al. (1988) reported the occurrence of hereditary nephritis, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa, and beta-thalassemia minor in 2 sibs with end-stage renal disease.

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) studied 3 MER2-negative patients of Indian Jewish origin with end-stage kidney disease, 2 of whom were sibs. In addition to hereditary nephritis, the sibs had sensorineural deafness, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa, and beta-thalassemia minor.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of EBS7 is consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance: Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) found the same CD151 mutation in homozygous state in 3 affected patients, 2 of whom were sibs. ⚠︎

### Mapping

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) noted that the CD151 gene is located at the same position on chromosome 11p15.5 as the MER2 blood group gene. They showed that CD151 expresses the MER2 blood group antigen and is present on erythrocytes.

### Molecular Genetics

In 3 MER2-negative patients of Indian Jewish origin with end-stage kidney disease, including 2 sibs who also had sensorineural deafness, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa, and beta-thalassemia minor, Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) identified homozygosity for a 1-bp insertion (G383) in exon 5 of the CD151 gene, causing a frameshift and a premature stop signal at codon 140. The resulting truncated protein would lack its integrin-binding domain.

### Pathogenesis

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) noted that CD151 forms very stable laminin-binding complexes with integrins alpha-3/beta-1 and alpha-6/beta-1 in kidney and alpha-3/beta-1 and alpha-6/beta-4 in skin. From their findings in patients with a truncating CD151 mutation, they concluded that CD151 is essential for the proper assembly of the glomerular and tubular basement membrane in kidney and has functional significance in skin, is probably a component of the inner ear, and could play a role in erythropoiesis.
