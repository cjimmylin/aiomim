### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that epidermolysis bullosa simplex-7 with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is caused by homozygous mutation in the CD151 gene (602243) on chromosome 11p15.5.

### Description

Epidermolysis bullosa simplex-7 with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is an autosomal recessive disorder. Its features are hereditary nephritis that progresses to end-stage renal disease, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa and sensorineural deafness. Patients lack the MER2 blood group antigen, which is carried by CD151 on erythrocytes (Karamatic Crew et al., 2004). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Kagan et al. (1988) reported 2 sibs with hereditary nephritis, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa and end-stage renal disease. Both sibs also had beta-thalassemia minor.

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) studied 3 MER2-negative patients of Indian Jewish origin who had end-stage kidney disease. Two of the patients were sibs. In addition to hereditary nephritis, the sibs had sensorineural deafness, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa and beta-thalassemia minor.

### Other Features

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) showed that CD151 is present on erythrocytes and carries the MER2 blood group antigen. Their patients were MER2-negative.

### Inheritance

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) found homozygous CD151 mutations in affected individuals, including a pair of sibs. This finding indicated that EBS7 is inherited in an autosomal recessive pattern. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) studied 3 MER2-negative patients of Indian Jewish origin with end-stage kidney disease, 2 of whom were sibs. All 3 were homozygous for a 1-bp insertion (G383) in exon 5 of the CD151 gene. The insertion caused a frameshift and a premature stop signal at codon 140. The authors predicted that the truncated protein would lack its integrin-binding domain.

### Pathogenesis

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) reported that CD151 forms stable laminin-binding complexes with integrins. They concluded that CD151 is essential for the correct assembly of the glomerular and tubular basement membranes in the kidney and that it has functional significance in the skin. They suggested that CD151 is probably a component of the inner ear and that it could play a role in erythropoiesis.
