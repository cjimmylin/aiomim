### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia, craniofacial abnormalities, and seizures (NEDHCS) is caused by homozygous mutation in the SNIP1 gene on chromosome 1p34.

### Description

Neurodevelopmental disorder with hypotonia, craniofacial abnormalities, and seizures is an autosomal recessive disorder. Its main features are severe global developmental delay or psychomotor retardation, hypotonia, seizures, and craniofacial dysmorphism. It was first described in the Old Order Amish. ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Puffenberger et al. (2012) studied Old Order Amish children with severe psychomotor retardation, hypotonia, epilepsy, and craniofacial dysmorphism. They used genetic mapping and exome sequencing to study this and several other new recessive disorders in Plain communities.

### Inheritance

Puffenberger et al. (2012) found homozygous mutations in affected children from Amish families, which is consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance.

### Molecular Genetics

In affected Old Order Amish children, Puffenberger et al. (2012) used homozygosity mapping and exome sequencing to identify a homozygous missense mutation in the SNIP1 gene (glu366-to-gly; E366G). The mutation segregated with the disorder. ⚠︎
