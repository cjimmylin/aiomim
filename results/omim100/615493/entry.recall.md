### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 (MRT37) is caused by homozygous mutation in the ANK3 gene (600465) on chromosome 10q21.2.

### Description

Autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by moderate intellectual disability, an attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)-like phenotype, and behavioral problems (Iqbal et al., 2013).

### Clinical Features

Iqbal et al. (2013) described a consanguineous family in which affected members had moderate intellectual disability, an ADHD-like phenotype, and behavioral problems.

### Inheritance

The finding of a homozygous ANK3 mutation in a consanguineous family by Iqbal et al. (2013) was compatible with autosomal recessive inheritance of MRT37.

### Molecular Genetics

In affected members of a consanguineous family with MRT37, Iqbal et al. (2013) identified a homozygous truncating frameshift mutation in the ANK3 gene. The mutation lay in the longest isoform of the gene. In the same report, Iqbal et al. (2013) described an unrelated patient with borderline intelligence and behavioral abnormalities in whom ANK3 was disrupted by a balanced translocation. They proposed that ANK3 disruption lies at the crossroads of neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disorders.
