### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 (MRT37) is caused by homozygous mutation in the ANK3 gene (600465) on chromosome 10q21.

### Description

Autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 is a neurodevelopmental disorder marked by severe intellectual disability. It comes with significant behavioral problems, including attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder and aggressive behavior. ✗

### Clinical Features

Iqbal et al. (2013) described a consanguineous Pakistani family in which several sibs had severe intellectual disability. Affected individuals also had attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder and behavioral problems, including aggression. Sleep disturbance was also reported. ✗

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of MRT37 in the family reported by Iqbal et al. (2013) fit autosomal recessive inheritance. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In affected members of a consanguineous Pakistani family with MRT37, Iqbal et al. (2013) found a homozygous truncating (frameshift) mutation in the ANK3 gene. The mutation lay in the large exon that is specific to the longest, neuron-specific isoform of ankyrin-G, so it was predicted to affect only that isoform. The mutation segregated with the disorder in the family. In the same report, Iqbal et al. (2013) described an unrelated patient with intellectual disability and behavioral abnormalities who carried a balanced translocation disrupting ANK3. They proposed that ANK3 disruption sits at the crossroads of neurodevelopmental and psychiatric disorders. ✗
