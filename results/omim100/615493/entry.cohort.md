### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-37 (MRT37) is caused by homozygous mutation in the ANK3 gene (600465) on chromosome 10q21.2. ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Iqbal et al. (2013) reported a consanguineous family in which affected members had moderate intellectual disability, an attention deficit-hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)-like phenotype, and behavioral problems.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of MRT37 in the consanguineous family reported by Iqbal et al. (2013), in which affected members carried a homozygous ANK3 mutation, was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In affected members of a consanguineous family with moderate intellectual disability, an ADHD-like phenotype, and behavioral problems, Iqbal et al. (2013) identified a homozygous truncating frameshift mutation in the longest isoform of the ANK3 gene. Iqbal et al. (2013) stated that this was the first reported familial mutation in the ANK3 gene. Memory defects in a Drosophila knockdown model of the ANK3 ortholog supported causality of the ANK3 mutation.

In the same report, Iqbal et al. (2013) described an unrelated patient with borderline intelligence, severe ADHD, autism, and sleeping problems in whom all isoforms of the ANK3 gene were disrupted by a balanced translocation, a heterozygous disruption of the gene.

### Animal Model

Iqbal et al. (2013) found memory defects in a Drosophila knockdown model of the ANK3 ortholog, which supported the causality of the ANK3 mutations in the 2 families they studied and a role for the gene in cognitive function.
