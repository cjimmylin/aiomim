## DYSTONIA, EARLY-ONSET, AND/OR SPASTIC PARAPLEGIA; DYTSPG

### TEXT

This entry describes a neurologic disorder in which affected individuals have dystonia, spastic paraplegia, or both (Neilson et al., 2022). Gilbert et al. (2009) reported onset of spasticity and weakness ranging from the first year to the sixth decade, and onset of dystonia from the first to the third decade.

### Description

Early-onset dystonia and/or spastic paraplegia (DYTSPG) is a neurologic disorder; Gilbert et al. (2009) concluded that it was a novel neurologic disorder. In the patients they described, spasticity and weakness began from the first year to the sixth decade, and dystonia from the first to the third decade. Neilson et al. (2022) described affected individuals with dystonia, spastic paraplegia with leg spasticity, or both. Neilson et al. (2022) found that severity differed between patients, from generalized dystonia to milder focal dystonia, and that the balance between dystonic and spastic features also differed, with some patients having mainly dystonia and others mainly spastic paraplegia. ⚠︎
