### Description

Common variable immunodeficiency (CVID) is a primary immunodeficiency. It is marked by low serum levels of IgG together with low IgA and/or IgM, and poor specific antibody responses to vaccination. Patients have recurrent bacterial infections, mainly of the respiratory and gastrointestinal tracts. Some develop autoimmunity, lymphoproliferation or granulomatous disease. ✗

### Heterogeneity

CVID is genetically heterogeneous, and several numbered forms are each linked to a different gene. CVID15 is one of these forms. ✗
