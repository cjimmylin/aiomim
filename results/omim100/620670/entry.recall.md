## IMMUNODEFICIENCY, COMMON VARIABLE, 15; CVID15

### TEXT

### Description

Schubert et al. (2018) noted that some primary antibody deficiencies are diagnosed as common variable immunodeficiency (CVID). They also noted that CVID patients whose only problem is infection make up a considerable subset of CVID cohorts.

### Heterogeneity

Schubert et al. (2018) stated that the known genetic causes of antibody deficiencies, some of which are diagnosed as CVID, affect one of three processes: early B-cell development, B-cell receptor signaling, or how B or T cells function in the germinal center reaction.
