# Proposed updates for OMIM 614701: CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4 WITH OR WITHOUT MIDLINE BRAIN DEFECTS

Literature from 2020 onwards that the entry does not cite: 26 papers screened, 6 read, 5 proposal(s). Each proposal is a draft for a curator to accept, edit or reject.

**Back-test as of 2020.** OMIM's curators later added 3 reference(s) from that year on; this run proposed 3 of them (recall 1.0), and 2 paper(s) OMIM does not cite.

## [MEDIUM] Krab et al. (2020) — larger_cohort, new_variants, phenotype_expansion, genotype_phenotype_correlation, functional_evidence, diagnostic_method

Krab LC, Marcos-Alcalde I, Assaf M, Balasubramanian M, Andersen JB, Bisgaard AM, Fitzpatrick DR, Gudmundsson S, Huisman SA, Kalayci T, and others. Delineation of phenotypes and genotypes related to cohesin structural protein RAD21. Hum Genet 139: 575-592, 2020. [PubMed: 32193685] *(read: fulltext)*

**Why:** Krab et al. (2020) describe the largest RAD21 cohort so far: 49 individuals from 33 families, including 24 new cases. The entry lacks feature frequencies, several new features (hearing loss, heart defects, cleft palate, vertebral and foregut anomalies, choanal and anal atresia, behavioral profile, postnatal microcephaly) and many variants not in the variant table (e.g., C585R, R586X, F600del, L603P, S345P, P376R, I206Tfs*3, G547Afs*65, T461I, R65Q). The paper also adds genotype-phenotype data comparing microdeletions with intragenic variants, molecular modeling, and evidence that familial transmission is common.

*clinical_features* ⚠︎: Krab et al. (2020) reported 49 individuals from 33 families with RAD21 alterations, including 24 previously unpublished cases. The patients came from Australia, Europe, Turkey, Canada, and the United States. Full clinical information was available for 29 individuals from 22 families (cohort A), all of whom had CdLS clinical scores of at least 5. Compared with the phenotype caused by NIPBL or SMC1A variants, the phenotype was attenuated, with milder facial morphology, limb anomalies, cognition, and behavior. Frequent features were short nose (23/26), long and/or smooth philtrum (26/29), concave nasal ridge (24/29), thick eyebrows (20/24), long eyelashes (21/26), synophrys (19/28), thin upper lip vermilion (23/29), short fifth finger (23/28), postnatal microcephaly (16/28), and gastroesophageal reflux (13/25). No patient had major limb malformations (0/29) or visual impairment (0/24). Small hands (5/27), small feet (3/27), hirsutism (10/26), and seizures (2/22) were less common than in the NIPBL and SMC1A cohorts. Hearing loss was present in 8/24 patients, heart defects in 9/23, and cleft palate in 6/25. Pregnancies and births were usually normal and prenatal growth retardation was uncommon; short stature and microcephaly developed after birth. About half of the patients had congenital anomalies. Unusual findings included vertebral anomalies (clefts and hemivertebrae) and malformations of structures derived from the embryonic foregut. Diaphragmatic hernia, anal atresia, or choanal atresia occurred occasionally. Sixteen of 29 patients (55%) had normal or mildly impaired cognition, and none had severe or profound disability. All patients aged 3 years and older used some words, and problems were mainly language-based. Behavioral problems were present in 14 of 25 (56%), mainly anxiety, ADHD, autistic features, and obsessive-compulsive behavior. Self-injurious behavior (1/18) and aggression (1/16) were infrequent.

*inheritance*: Krab et al. (2020) noted that RAD21 variants were frequently familial: 9 of 21 index cases with known inheritance had familial variants, as did 5 of 12 cohort A cases with known inheritance. Mildly affected parents were sometimes diagnosed only after more severely affected offspring had been recognized. The authors therefore recommended parental testing even when parents appear unaffected.

*heterogeneity*: From limited data on a second cohort (cohort B), Krab et al. (2020) suggested that biallelic RAD21 variants can cause Mungan syndrome and that monoallelic RAD21 variants can cause holoprosencephaly with or without CdLS characteristics. In several further cases, including some with sclerocornea, it was uncertain whether the RAD21 variant was involved.

*molecular_genetics*: Krab et al. (2020) identified 31 different RAD21 alterations in 33 families. These comprised 7 unique deletions, 6 of which also included other genes, and 24 intragenic sequence variants. Of the sequence variants, 13 were predicted to be truncating (2 nonsense, 2 splice site, and 9 frameshift) and were scattered throughout the gene. Three were in-frame deletions, and the missense variants tended to cluster at the protein-binding domains. Two variants were recurrent: a c.1753T-C transition resulting in a cys585-to-arg (C585R) substitution, and a c.1756C-T transition resulting in an arg586-to-ter (R586X) substitution. C585R arose de novo in 1 previously reported patient and was found in 2 relatives in a new family. Molecular dynamics modeling predicted that C585R destabilizes the RAD21-SMC1A domain by causing the adjacent arg586 to lose its salt-bridge contact with glu577. R586X was found in 2 previously unpublished families; in one family it was inherited from the father, who was also affected, and facial morphology differed between the families.

*molecular_genetics* ⚠︎: Krab et al. (2020) reported further RAD21 variants in CdLS patients. In a previously unpublished patient with classic CdLS and no variants in other known CdLS genes on a targeted gene panel, they found an in-frame deletion, c.1800_1802del, resulting in deletion of phe600 (F600del). Modeling showed no structural effect, but the authors considered the variant likely pathogenic on the basis of the phenotype. In a previously reported patient, they found a de novo c.1808T-C transition resulting in a leu603-to-pro (L603P) substitution; modeling predicted that pro603 bends the alpha helix and moves lys605 out of its position near ATPase site 1. In a DECIPHER patient, they found a de novo c.1033T-C transition resulting in a ser345-to-pro (S345P) substitution. Modeling predicted that S345P impairs the interaction between RAD21 and STAG1/2 by creating a new curved alpha-helix segment that separates from the STAG2 surface. In contrast, the previously reported de novo c.1127C-G transversion, resulting in a pro376-to-arg (P376R) substitution, was predicted to form a new salt bridge that over-stabilizes the RAD21-STAG2 interaction. Other previously unpublished heterozygous variants were 2 de novo frameshifts, c.617_620del (Ile206ThrfsTer3) and c.1635del (Gly547AlafsTer65), and a paternally inherited c.1382C-T transition resulting in a thr461-to-ile (T461I) substitution. The authors also included a c.194G-A transition resulting in an arg65-to-gln (R65Q) substitution, which was listed in ClinVar as a variant of uncertain significance. Based on phenotype, modeling, and the literature, they considered R65Q and T461I likely pathogenic, possibly acting through altered phosphorylation motifs or PDS5 interaction.

*molecular_genetics*: Krab et al. (2020) updated the family reported by Boyle et al. (2017) with the c.704delG (Ser235IlefsTer19) variant. The variant was carried by 5 affected members, whose CdLS scores ranged from 9 to 12, and facial morphology varied within the family. In the patient with the de novo gln592 deletion (Q592del) reported by Gudmundsson et al. (2019), whose CdLS score was 8, molecular dynamics simulation predicted that loss of gln592 distorts an alpha helix and moves lys605 away from ATPase site 1 of the SMC1A/SMC3 head, disturbing the cohesin cycle.

*genotype_phenotype_correlations*: Compared with patients carrying intragenic variants, Krab et al. (2020) observed a trend toward higher CdLS scores and more frequent growth impairment in patients with microdeletions. They found no difference in major malformations or in cognitive and behavioral problems. CdLS scores did not differ between truncating and nontruncating sequence variants. Variability within and between families was striking, especially in cognition and behavior, and in most families it could not be explained by mosaicism.

*pathogenesis*: Krab et al. (2020) suggested that the relatively mild phenotype indicates that, unlike other CdLS genes, RAD21 is not highly intolerant to loss of function. They speculated that RAD21 haploinsufficiency may be compensated more effectively than loss of other cohesin genes. They noted that haploinsufficiency gave roughly halved RAD21 RNA levels without compensatory upregulation.

*diagnosis*: Krab et al. (2020) reported that no RAD21 variant in their cohort would have been missed using the CdLS consensus criteria for molecular testing. CdLS had been suspected before testing in about 60% of index cases (13/21), and the authors concluded that the CdLS score remains a reliable tool.

## [MEDIUM] Lei et al. (2020) — phenotype_expansion, new_variants, genotype_phenotype_correlation, diagnostic_method

Lei M, Liang D, Yang Y, Mitsuhashi S, Katoh K, Miyake N, Frith MC, Wu L, Matsumoto N. Long-read DNA sequencing fully characterized chromothripsis in a patient with Langer-Giedion syndrome and Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4. J Hum Genet 65: 667-674, 2020. [PubMed: 32296131] *(read: fulltext)*

**Why:** This paper is the first in the entry to describe RAD21 deficiency caused by germline chromothripsis. It was resolved by nanopore long-read sequencing and produced a contiguous deletion of RAD21 and EXT1 that spared TRPS1, with reduced RAD21 expression. It also adds features the entry does not yet list: congenital heart defect, cleft palate, thoracic scoliosis, butterfly vertebrae, umbilical hernia and multiple exostoses. It also compares 6 patients with RAD21/EXT1 deletions.

*clinical_features*: Lei et al. (2020) described a 6-year-old Han Chinese boy, the first child of healthy unrelated parents, who had a de novo t(8;18)(q22;q21) translocation. He had growth retardation (height and weight -2.0 SD), mild-to-moderate intellectual disability, left-sided cleft palate, and a congenital heart defect (ventricular septal defect, double-outlet right ventricle, and juxtaposition of the aorta and pulmonary artery). Other features were thoracic scoliosis, butterfly vertebrae, fifth-finger clinodactyly, fingerpads, umbilical and inguinal hernias, and recurrent respiratory infections. Facial dysmorphism included arched thick eyebrows, long eyelashes, a depressed nasal bridge, anteverted nostrils, large prominent ears, dental malocclusion, and micrognathia. Kabuki syndrome was initially suspected. Multiple exostoses were found at age 10 years.

*cytogenetics*: Lei et al. (2020) used nanopore long-read sequencing and the dnarrange pipeline to study a boy with features of CdLS4 and Langer-Giedion syndrome. They showed that his de novo t(8;18) translocation was germline chromothripsis: the genome had been shattered into 19 fragments (15 from chromosome 8 and 4 from chromosome 18) that rejoined in random order and orientation. The rearrangement caused 4 deletions on chromosome 8 (456, 1,957, 520, and 3,529 kb) and 1 deletion on chromosome 18 (1,528 kb), all consistent with array results. One chromosome 8 deletion contained RAD21 and EXT1 but not TRPS1. Earlier short-read whole-genome sequencing had not resolved the rearrangement, and no pathogenic variants were found in the Kabuki syndrome genes.

*molecular_genetics*: In lymphoblastoid cells from a boy with a chromothripsis-associated deletion that included RAD21 and EXT1, Lei et al. (2020) found reduced RAD21 and EXT1 expression by quantitative RT-PCR, whereas TRPS1 expression was unchanged. They concluded that loss of RAD21 and EXT1 explained the phenotype, likely a contiguous gene syndrome combining CdLS4 and atypical Langer-Giedion syndrome. They considered involvement of other disrupted genes, including ZFPM2, unlikely.

*genotype_phenotype_correlations*: Lei et al. (2020) compared their patient with 5 previously reported patients who had Langer-Giedion syndrome with deletions that included RAD21 and EXT1 but spared TRPS1. The 6 patients shared overlapping features: arched thick eyebrows (6 patients), long eyelashes (5), depressed or broad nasal bridge (5), sparse scalp hair (4), and intellectual disability (4 of 6). Only their patient had thoracic scoliosis and a congenital heart defect. Because 2 of 8 previously reported patients with RAD21 variants had congenital heart disease, the authors suggested that RAD21 deficiency might be responsible for the heart disease.

## [LOW] Goel and Parasivam (2020) — new_variants

Goel H, Parasivam G. Another case of holoprosencephaly associated with RAD21 loss-of-function variant. Brain 143: e64, 2020. [PubMed: 32696056] *(read: title)*

**Why:** This paper adds one more case of holoprosencephaly with a RAD21 loss-of-function variant. Kruszka et al. (2019) already link RAD21 to holoprosencephaly, and only the title is available, so the specific variant, zygosity and clinical details are unknown. It is a minor confirmatory addition.

*molecular_genetics*: Goel and Parasivam (2020) reported another case of holoprosencephaly associated with a loss-of-function variant in the RAD21 gene.

## [LOW] Dorval et al. (2020) — new_variants

Dorval S, Masciadri M, Mathot M, Russo S, Revencu N, Larizza L. A novel RAD21 mutation in a boy with mild Cornelia de Lange presentation: Further delineation of the phenotype. Eur J Med Genet 63: 103620, 2020. [PubMed: 30716475] *(read: abstract)*

**Why:** Dorval et al. (2020) add one more boy with mild CdLS and a novel RAD21 mutation. The abstract does not give the variant, so it cannot be added to the variant table. The point that RAD21 mutations cause a milder phenotype is already in the entry (Deardorff et al., 2012; Minor et al., 2014), so this is at most a minor added case.

*clinical_features*: Dorval et al. (2020) reported a boy with a mild Cornelia de Lange syndrome presentation and a molecularly confirmed novel RAD21 mutation. They compared his clinical features with those of previously reported patients carrying other RAD21 intragenic mutations to further delineate the phenotype, and noted that RAD21 mutations are associated with a milder phenotype.

## [LOW] Herrero-García et al. (2020) — phenotype_expansion

Herrero-García A, Marín-Reina P, Cabezuelo-Huerta G, Ferrer-Lorente MB, Rosello M, Orellana C, Martínez F, Pérez-Aytés A. Mixed Phenotype of Langer-Giedion's and Cornelia de Lange's Syndromes in an 8q23.3-q24.1 Microdeletion without TRPS1 Deletion. J Pediatr Genet 9: 53-57, 2020. [PubMed: 31976145] *(read: abstract)*

**Why:** This paper adds one patient with a 2.3-Mb 8q23.3-q24.1 deletion. The deletion explicitly includes RAD21 and EXT1 but not TRPS1, and the patient had a mixed Langer-Giedion/CdLS4 phenotype. The 8q24 deletions already in the entry are not described as including RAD21 or as giving a CdLS4 phenotype, so this is a minor cytogenetic addition.

*cytogenetics*: Herrero-García et al. (2020) reported a female patient with a 2.3-Mb interstitial deletion of chromosome 8q23.3-q24.1. The deletion included the EXT1 and RAD21 genes but not TRPS1. The authors attributed her clinical findings to a mixed phenotype of Langer-Giedion syndrome (trichorhinophalangeal syndrome type II) and Cornelia de Lange syndrome type 4.
