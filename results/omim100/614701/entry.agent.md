### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 with or without midline brain defects (CDLS4) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the RAD21 gene (606462) on chromosome 8q24.

### Description

Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 (CDLS4) is a cohesinopathy caused by mutation in the gene for the cohesin structural protein RAD21. Affected individuals have growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features that overlap those of classic Cornelia de Lange syndrome (CdLS), but cognitive impairment is milder than in CdLS caused by mutation in NIPBL, SMC1A, or SMC3 (Deardorff et al., 2012). Compared with NIPBL- and SMC1A-related CdLS, the phenotype is attenuated for facial morphology, limb anomalies, and especially cognition and behavior; some patients have holoprosencephaly, with or without CdLS features, and variants are frequently familial with marked intra- and interfamilial variability (Krab et al., 2020).

### Clinical Features

Deardorff et al. (2012) reported children with RAD21 mutations who had growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features overlapping those of CdLS. Unlike children with mutations in NIPBL, SMC1A, or SMC3, these individuals had much milder cognitive impairment than those with classic CdLS.

Minor et al. (2014) reported 2 patients with atypical CdLS. The first patient had developmental delay, hypospadias, inguinal hernia, and dysmorphic features; his mother, from whom the mutation was inherited, had a history of melanoma and other unspecified medical problems. The second patient had developmental delay, characteristic facial features, hirsutism, and hand and feet anomalies; the first patient was milder than the second.

Boyle et al. (2017) reported a patient (IV.16) with mild CdLS whose mother and 2 aunts carried the same RAD21 variant; none of these relatives had been suspected of having CdLS or a cohesinopathy, and they did not fulfill the diagnostic criteria for CdLS. The authors suggested incomplete penetrance associated with RAD21 variants and possible underdiagnosis of such individuals.

Gudmundsson et al. (2019) reported a 15-month-old boy with developmental delay, distinct CdLS-like facial features, gastrointestinal reflux in early infancy, testis retention, prominent digit pads, and diaphragmatic hernia.

Kruszka et al. (2019) reported holoprosencephaly associated with variants in cohesin complex genes, including 4 individuals with variants in the non-X-linked genes SMC3 and RAD21.

Dorval et al. (2020) described a boy with a mild CdLS presentation and a molecularly confirmed RAD21 mutation and compared his features with those of previously reported patients carrying RAD21 intragenic mutations.

Krab et al. (2020) assembled a series of 49 individuals from 33 families with RAD21 alterations, including 24 previously unpublished cases; full clinical data were available for 29 individuals (22 families; cohort A; age range 0-61 years, median 9 years). All cohort A patients had CdLS clinical scores of at least 5, and CdLS had been suspected before testing in 13 of 21 index cases in whom this was specified. Most CdLS features were present but at lower frequency and severity than in NIPBL and SMC1A cohorts: major limb malformations were not seen (0/29), hands and feet were generally of normal size, and hirsutism was less frequent (38%). Frequent features included short nose (88%), long and/or smooth philtrum (90%), concave nasal ridge (83%), thick eyebrows (83%), long eyelashes (81%), short fifth finger (82%), and postnatal microcephaly (57%). Growth at birth was generally less impaired, with short stature and microcephaly developing postnatally. Hearing loss was found in 8 of 24, cardiac anomalies in 9 of 23, gastroesophageal reflux (typically mild) in 13 of 25, and seizures in 2 of 22. Unusual anomalies included vertebral clefts and hemivertebrae and malformations of foregut-derived structures. Sixteen of 29 (55%) had normal or mildly impaired cognition, none had severe or profound disability, and all aged 3 years and above used some words. Behavioral problems, mainly anxiety, ADHD, autism spectrum features, and obsessive-compulsive behavior, were present in 14 of 25; self-injurious behavior and aggression were uncommon. Intrafamilial variability, especially in cognition and behavior, was considerable, and some mildly affected parents were diagnosed only after recognition of more severely affected offspring. ⚠︎

Krab et al. (2020) noted that holoprosencephaly spectrum anomalies had been linked to several cohesin genes, including RAD21, and that 1 patient in their cohort A with holoprosencephaly carried a monoallelic RAD21 variant; the prevalence of holoprosencephaly spectrum anomalies in RAD21-CdLS remained uncertain because brain MRIs are typically not performed in CdLS.

### Inheritance

Deardorff et al. (2012) described dominant missense mutations in RAD21. Familial transmission of heterozygous RAD21 variants with variable expression and apparent incomplete penetrance was reported by Minor et al. (2014) and Boyle et al. (2017). In the series of Krab et al. (2020), variants were familial in 9 of 21 index cases for whom inheritance could be established. ⚠︎

### Cytogenetics

Wuyts et al. (2002) described a boy with multiple exostoses, hypertrichosis, mental retardation, and epilepsy due to a de novo deletion on chromosome 8q24 that overlapped the Langer-Giedion syndrome region, involving the EXT1 gene and additional genes distal to it but probably not TRPS1. Krab et al. (2020) listed this case among excluded published cases with involvement of other morbid genes.

McBrien et al. (2008) reported a boy with a small 8q24 microdeletion characterized by oligo array CGH that involved EXT1 but not TRPS1, with a mild Langer-Giedion-like phenotype and unusual features including prominent toe pads and fat pads on the soles of his feet. Krab et al. (2020) listed this patient as identical to patient 2 of Deardorff et al. (2012), carrying a de novo 1.7-Mb deletion at 8q23.3-q24.12 encompassing RAD21.

Krab et al. (2020) noted that RAD21 lies at 8q24.11 between TRPS1 and EXT1, and that several microdeletions involving RAD21 also encompass neighboring genes, complicating attribution of the phenotype to RAD21. In their series, all 7 copy number variants were deletions, 6 of which included genes other than RAD21; exostoses related to EXT1 haploinsufficiency likely caused the upper limb anomalies in patients with microdeletions.

### Molecular Genetics

Deardorff et al. (2012) identified mutations in RAD21 in children with a CdLS-like cohesinopathy. The mutations acted at the RAD21 interface with the cohesin proteins STAG2 and SMC1A, impaired the cellular DNA damage response, and disrupted transcription in a zebrafish model. As tabulated by Krab et al. (2020), the patients of Deardorff et al. (2012) included patient 5 with a de novo pro376-to-arg (P376R) mutation, patient 6 with a de novo cys585-to-arg (C585R) mutation, and patients 2 and 4 with whole-gene deletions.

Minor et al. (2014) reported the first intragenic deletion and frameshift mutations in RAD21 in 2 patients with atypical CdLS: an in-frame deletion of exon 13, inherited from the mother, and a c.592_593dup frameshift mutation.

In a patient with mild CdLS, Boyle et al. (2017) identified a heterozygous 1-bp deletion (c.704delG) in RAD21, predicted to cause a premature stop codon (Ser235IlefsTer19). The deletion was also found in the patient's mother and 2 aunts. ⚠︎

By exome sequencing in a 15-month-old boy with CDLS4, Gudmundsson et al. (2019) identified a de novo 3-bp deletion (c.1774_1776del) in RAD21, resulting in deletion of gln592 (Gln592del). In silico structural modeling predicted loss of contact between RAD21 lys591 and SMC1A residues glu1191 and glu1192, altering the RAD21-SMC1A interface.

Kruszka et al. (2019) reported RAD21 variants among individuals with holoprosencephaly. As tabulated by Krab et al. (2020), patient 14 of Kruszka et al. (2019) carried a de novo 8-bp deletion (c.1217_1224del; Lys406ArgfsTer4) and patient 12 carried a paternally inherited 1-bp duplication (c.1550dupC; Glu518ArgfsTer19). Krab et al. (2020) noted that some individuals with holoprosencephaly and cohesin gene loss-of-function variants had CdLS features, but that in some cases the relationship between phenotype and RAD21 variant could not be confirmed.

Goel and Parasivam (2020) reported another case of holoprosencephaly associated with a RAD21 loss-of-function variant.

Dorval et al. (2020) reported a novel RAD21 mutation in a boy with mild CdLS. As tabulated by Krab et al. (2020), this patient carried a de novo 4-bp deletion (c.943_946del; Glu315GlnfsTer9).

Krab et al. (2020) reported 31 different RAD21 alterations in 33 families: 7 unique deletions and 24 intragenic sequence variants, 2 of which (C585R and R586X) were each found in 2 families. Of the sequence variants, 13 were predicted to be truncating and were scattered throughout the gene, 3 were in-frame deletions, and missense variants tended to cluster in the protein-binding domains. Protein modeling and molecular dynamics analysis of 12 intragenic variants indicated that at least 6 (including C585R and Gln592del) were likely pathogenic, with C585R and R586Q destabilizing the RAD21-SMC1A domain. The authors also noted that biallelic RAD21 variants (homozygous A622T) were found in a family with Mungan syndrome, some members of which had CdLS features. ⚠︎

### Genotype/Phenotype Correlations

Deardorff et al. (2012) suggested that, compared with loss-of-function mutations, dominant missense mutations in RAD21 cause more severe functional defects and worse structural and cognitive clinical findings.

In contrast, Krab et al. (2020) found no difference in CdLS scores or growth parameters between individuals with truncating and nontruncating sequence variants, and stated that patients with haploinsufficiency due to microdeletions or truncating variants did not differ markedly from those with missense variants. There was a trend toward higher CdLS scores and more frequently impaired growth in patients with microdeletions than in those with intragenic variants, without differences in major malformations or cognitive and behavioral problems. Striking interfamilial and intrafamilial variability was observed, e.g., among patients carrying the R586X variant.

### Diagnosis

Krab et al. (2020) found that no RAD21 variant in their cohort would have been missed using the CdLS consensus criteria for molecular testing, and concluded that the CdLS score remains a reliable tool. They recommended parental testing even if parents appear unaffected, because of the high frequency of familial occurrence and marked variability.

### Animal Model

Deardorff et al. (2012) found that RAD21 mutations disrupted transcription in a zebrafish model.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, PRO376ARG [c.1127C>G; p.Pro376Arg]

In patient 5 of Deardorff et al. (2012), Krab et al. (2020) tabulated a de novo c.1127C-G transversion in exon 9 of the RAD21 gene, resulting in a pro376-to-arg (P376R) substitution in the RAD21-STAG domain. Protein modeling predicted that the variant forms a new salt bridge between RAD21 and STAG2, over-stabilizing their interaction.

**.0002 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, CYS585ARG [c.1753T>C; p.Cys585Arg]

In patient 6 of Deardorff et al. (2012), Krab et al. (2020) tabulated a de novo c.1753T-C transition in exon 14 of the RAD21 gene, resulting in a cys585-to-arg (C585R) substitution. Krab et al. (2020) found the same variant in 2 members of an unrelated family (parents not tested). Molecular dynamics modeling showed that C585R causes the adjacent arg586 to lose its interaction with glu577, destabilizing the RAD21-SMC1A domain.

**.0003 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, 665-BP DEL, EX13 [c.1621-388_1704+193del; p.Asp541_Gln568del]

In a patient with atypical CdLS with developmental delay, hypospadias, inguinal hernia, and dysmorphic features, Minor et al. (2014) identified an in-frame deletion of exon 13 of the RAD21 gene, inherited from his mother. Krab et al. (2020) specified this as a 665-bp deletion (c.1621-388_1704+193del) resulting in an in-frame deletion of residues asp541 to gln568.

**.0004 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**

In a patient with atypical CdLS with developmental delay, characteristic facial features, hirsutism, and hand and feet anomalies, Minor et al. (2014) identified a c.592_593dup frameshift mutation in the RAD21 gene. Krab et al. (2020) listed this variant in exon 6 as c.592_593dupAG, predicted to result in Ser198ArgfsTer6.

**.0005 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, 1-BP DEL, 704G [c.704delG; p.Ser235IlefsTer19]

In a patient with mild CdLS, Boyle et al. (2017) identified a heterozygous 1-bp deletion (c.704delG) in the RAD21 gene, predicted to result in a frameshift and premature stop codon (Ser235IlefsTer19). The deletion was also present in the patient's mother and 2 aunts, who did not fulfill the diagnostic criteria for CdLS. ⚠︎

**.0006 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**  
RAD21, GLN592DEL [c.1774_1776del; p.Gln592del]

In a 15-month-old boy with CDLS4 and diaphragmatic hernia, Gudmundsson et al. (2019) identified a de novo 3-bp deletion (c.1774_1776del) in the RAD21 gene, resulting in deletion of gln592. The variant was found by exome sequencing and was absent in both healthy parents. Structural modeling predicted disruption of the RAD21-SMC1A interface. Krab et al. (2020) found by molecular dynamics that the deletion displaces lys605 away from ATPase site 1.

**.0007 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4 WITH HOLOPROSENCEPHALY**

In patient 14 of Kruszka et al. (2019), Krab et al. (2020) tabulated a de novo 8-bp deletion (c.1217_1224del) in exon 10 of the RAD21 gene, predicted to cause a frameshift (Lys406ArgfsTer4); the patient had a CdLS score of 13.

**.0008 CORNELIA DE LANGE SYNDROME 4**

In a boy with a mild CdLS presentation reported by Dorval et al. (2020), Krab et al. (2020) tabulated a de novo 4-bp deletion (c.943_946del) in exon 9 of the RAD21 gene, predicted to cause a frameshift (Glu315GlnfsTer9).
