A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 with or without midline brain defects (CDLS4) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the RAD21 gene (606462) on chromosome 8q24.11.

### Description

Cornelia de Lange syndrome-4 (CDLS4) is a cohesinopathy caused by heterozygous mutation in RAD21 (Krab et al., 2020), which encodes an integral structural protein of the cohesin complex (Deardorff et al., 2012). Affected individuals have features that overlap those of classic Cornelia de Lange syndrome, including growth retardation, facial features, and minor skeletal anomalies, but their cognitive impairment is much milder than in classic CdLS (Deardorff et al., 2012). Some patients have midline brain defects in the holoprosencephaly spectrum (Krab et al., 2020).

### Clinical Features

Deardorff et al. (2012) described individuals with RAD21 mutations. Their features overlapped with those of Cornelia de Lange syndrome and included growth retardation, minor skeletal anomalies, and facial features that overlapped those seen in CdLS. Unlike children with mutations in NIPBL, SMC1A, or SMC3, these individuals had much milder cognitive impairment than those with classic CdLS.

Kruszka et al. (2019) found variants in cohesin complex genes, including RAD21, in patients with holoprosencephaly. They concluded that these findings showed the cohesin complex to be an important regulator of median forebrain development.

### Inheritance

Deardorff et al. (2012) described the RAD21 missense mutations in their patients as dominant.

### Heterogeneity

Cornelia de Lange syndrome is genetically heterogeneous (Deardorff et al., 2012). Krab et al. (2020) noted that variants in other genes encoding structural or functional components of the cohesin complex, including NIPBL, SMC1A, SMC3, and HDAC8, have been implicated in CdLS.

### Molecular Genetics

Deardorff et al. (2012) identified mutations in the RAD21 gene in patients with a congenital phenotype consistent with a cohesinopathy that overlapped with CdLS. Mechanistically, the mutations acted at the interface of RAD21 with the other cohesin proteins STAG2 and SMC1A, and they impaired the cellular DNA damage response.

In a study of cohesin-associated holoprosencephaly, Kruszka et al. (2019) identified RAD21 variants in patients with holoprosencephaly. ⚠︎
