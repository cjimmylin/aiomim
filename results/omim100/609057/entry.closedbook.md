A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that epidermolysis bullosa simplex with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is caused by homozygous mutation in the CD151 gene on chromosome 11p15.

### Description

Epidermolysis bullosa simplex with nephropathy and deafness is an autosomal recessive disorder. Its main features are pretibial blistering of the skin, hereditary nephropathy that progresses to end-stage renal disease, and sensorineural deafness. Affected individuals lack the CD151 tetraspanin. Because CD151 carries the MER2 (RAPH) blood group antigen, they are MER2-negative. ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) studied 3 MER2-negative individuals from 2 families. All 3 had end-stage hereditary nephritis, sensorineural deafness and pretibial epidermolysis bullosa. Skin and kidney findings pointed to defective assembly of basement membranes, including those of the glomerulus. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

The disorder is inherited in an autosomal recessive pattern. Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) found the causative CD151 mutation in the homozygous state in affected individuals. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) found a homozygous single-nucleotide insertion in the CD151 gene in MER2-negative patients with nephropathy, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa and deafness. The insertion caused a frameshift and a truncated protein that lacked the MER2 antigen. The authors concluded that CD151 is needed for the correct assembly of basement membranes in kidney, skin and the inner ear. ⚠︎

Vahidnezhad et al. (2018) reported biallelic CD151 mutations in a patient with epidermolysis bullosa simplex. This finding widened the range of skin phenotypes linked to CD151 deficiency. ✗
