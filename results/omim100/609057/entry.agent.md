### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that epidermolysis bullosa simplex-7 with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is caused by homozygous mutation in the CD151 gene on chromosome 11p15.5.

### Description

Epidermolysis bullosa simplex-7 with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is characterized by hereditary nephritis progressing to end-stage kidney disease, sensorineural deafness, and pretibial epidermolysis bullosa. Affected individuals lack the MER2 blood group antigen, which is carried by the tetraspanin CD151 (Kagan et al., 1988; Karamatic Crew et al., 2004).

### Clinical Features

Kagan et al. (1988) reported the occurrence of hereditary nephritis, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa, and beta-thalassemia minor in 2 sibs with end-stage renal disease.

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) studied 3 MER2-negative patients of Indian Jewish origin with end-stage kidney disease, 2 of whom were sibs. In addition to hereditary nephritis, the sibs had sensorineural deafness, pretibial epidermolysis bullosa, and beta-thalassemia minor. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

The homozygosity for a CD151 mutation in affected patients, 2 of whom were sibs, reported by Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) indicated autosomal recessive inheritance of this disorder. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) showed that CD151, a tetraspanin that forms stable laminin-binding complexes with integrins in kidney and skin and is encoded by a gene at the same position on chromosome 11p15.5 as the MER2 blood group gene, expresses the MER2 blood group antigen and is located on erythrocytes. In 3 MER2-negative patients of Indian Jewish origin with end-stage kidney disease, 2 of whom were sibs, they identified homozygosity for a 1-bp insertion (G383) in exon 5 of the CD151 gene, causing a frameshift and a premature stop signal at codon 140. The resulting truncated protein would lack its integrin-binding domain. The authors concluded that CD151 is essential for the proper assembly of the glomerular and tubular basement membrane in kidney, has functional significance in the skin, is probably a component of the inner ear, and could play a role in erythropoiesis.
