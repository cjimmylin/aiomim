### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that epidermolysis bullosa simplex with nephropathy and deafness (EBS7) is caused by homozygous mutation in the CD151 gene (602243) on chromosome 11p15.5. ⚠︎

### Description

Epidermolysis bullosa simplex with nephropathy and deafness is characterized by pretibial blistering of the skin (pretibial epidermolysis bullosa), hereditary nephritis that progresses to end-stage kidney disease, and sensorineural deafness (Karamatic Crew et al., 2004). CD151 expresses the MER2 blood group antigen, and affected individuals are MER2-negative. Affected individuals are homozygous for a CD151 mutation predicted to produce a truncated protein that would lack its integrin-binding domain (Karamatic Crew et al., 2004). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) studied 3 MER2-negative patients from 2 families, 2 of whom were sibs. All 3 had end-stage kidney disease due to hereditary nephritis. The sibs also had sensorineural deafness and pretibial epidermolysis bullosa. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) found the CD151 mutation in the homozygous state in affected individuals, 2 of whom were sibs, a finding consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In 3 MER2-negative patients with hereditary nephritis, 2 of whom were sibs who also had pretibial epidermolysis bullosa and deafness, Karamatic Crew et al. (2004) identified a homozygous single-nucleotide insertion in the CD151 gene that caused a frameshift. The resulting truncated protein would lack its integrin-binding domain. The authors concluded that CD151 is essential for the proper assembly of the glomerular and tubular basement membrane in the kidney, has functional significance in the skin, and is probably a component of the inner ear.
