### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that orofaciodigital syndrome XVIII (OFD18) is caused by homozygous mutation in the IFT57 gene on chromosome 3q13.

### Description

Orofaciodigital syndrome XVIII is an autosomal recessive ciliopathy. Like the other forms of orofaciodigital syndrome, it is defined by abnormalities of the oral cavity, face, and digits, including features such as oral frenulae, cleft lip and/or palate, and polydactyly.

### Inheritance

OFD18 is inherited in an autosomal recessive pattern, as shown by the homozygous IFT57 mutation found in the reported consanguineous family (Thevenon et al., 2016).

### Heterogeneity

Orofaciodigital syndromes are a genetically heterogeneous group of disorders. Most of the known causal genes encode proteins that act at the primary cilium or the centrosome.

### Molecular Genetics

Thevenon et al. (2016) performed whole-exome sequencing in a consanguineous family with an orofaciodigital syndrome phenotype. They found a homozygous mutation in the IFT57 gene, which encodes a component of the intraflagellar transport complex B. This tied the disorder to defective ciliary intraflagellar transport.
