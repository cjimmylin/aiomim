### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that orofaciodigital syndrome XVIII (OFD18) is caused by homozygous mutation in the IFT57 gene (606621) on chromosome 3q13.12-q13.13.

### Description

Orofaciodigital syndrome XVIII is an autosomal recessive ciliopathy (Thevenon et al., 2016). The orofaciodigital syndromes are a heterogeneous group of disorders that share malformations of the oral cavity, face, and digits (Phadke et al., 1999). These can include oral frenulae, cleft lip and/or palate, and polydactyly (Thevenon et al., 2016).

### Inheritance

Thevenon et al. (2016) found a homozygous IFT57 mutation in a consanguineous family with OFD18, consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance.

### Heterogeneity

Thevenon et al. (2016) noted that the orofaciodigital syndromes are genetically heterogeneous, and that most of the known causal genes encode proteins that act at the primary cilium or the centrosome. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Thevenon et al. (2016) performed whole-exome sequencing in a consanguineous family with an orofaciodigital syndrome phenotype. They identified a homozygous mutation in the IFT57 gene, which encodes a component of intraflagellar transport complex B. Thevenon et al. (2016) concluded that this finding tied the disorder to defective ciliary intraflagellar transport.
