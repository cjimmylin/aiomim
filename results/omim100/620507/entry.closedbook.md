### Description

Ichthyosis with erythrokeratoderma (IEKD) is a disorder of keratinization. It is characterized by generalized ichthyotic scaling together with well-demarcated erythematous hyperkeratotic plaques of the erythrokeratoderma type. Palmoplantar keratoderma may also be present.
