### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal dominant distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-11 (HMND11) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the SPTAN1 gene (182810) on chromosome 9q34.

### Description

Autosomal dominant distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-11 (HMND11) is a form of distal hereditary motor neuropathy. Beijer et al. (2019) described the distal hereditary motor neuropathies as a rare subgroup of inherited peripheral neuropathies, hallmarked by length-dependent axonal degeneration of lower motor neurons without significant involvement of sensory neurons. Beijer et al. (2019) reported juvenile onset in HMND11.

### Clinical Features

Beijer et al. (2019) described 3 unrelated families with autosomal dominant hereditary motor neuropathy of juvenile onset.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of HMND11 in the 3 families reported by Beijer et al. (2019) was autosomal dominant.

### Molecular Genetics

Beijer et al. (2019) used next-generation sequencing in 3 unrelated families with dominant juvenile-onset hereditary motor neuropathy and found heterozygous nonsense mutations in the SPTAN1 gene, which encodes alpha-II spectrin. Penetrance of the mutations varied in 2 of the 3 families.
