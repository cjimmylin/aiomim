### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive dilated cardiomyopathy-2A (CMD2A) is caused by mutation in the gene encoding cardiac troponin I (TNNI3; 191044) on chromosome 19q13.42.

### Description

Murphy et al. (2004) noted that idiopathic dilated cardiomyopathy is a common cause of heart failure. CMD2A is an autosomal recessive form of idiopathic dilated cardiomyopathy caused by mutation in the cardiac troponin I gene (TNNI3) (Murphy et al., 2004).

### Inheritance

Murphy et al. (2004) established autosomal recessive inheritance of CMD2A. They identified a TNNI3 mutation in a family with recessive disease.

### Heterogeneity

Carballo et al. (2009) noted that dilated cardiomyopathy is genetically heterogeneous and that inherited forms are usually transmitted as autosomal dominant traits. TNNI3 is a known hypertrophic cardiomyopathy disease gene, and Carballo et al. (2009) reported the first autosomal dominant dilated cardiomyopathy-causing mutations in TNNI3.

### Molecular Genetics

Murphy et al. (2004) studied a family with recessively inherited idiopathic dilated cardiomyopathy and identified a novel mutation in the TNNI3 gene. Functional studies showed impairment of troponin interactions that could lead to diminished myocardial contractility.
