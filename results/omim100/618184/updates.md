## [MEDIUM] Smit et al. (2008) — new_variants, natural_history

Smit LS, Roofthooft D, van Ruissen F, Baas F, van Doorn PA. Congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy, a long term follow-up study in an affected family. Neuromuscul Disord 18: 59-62, 2008. [PubMed: 17825553] *(read: abstract)*

**Why:** Smit et al. (2008) report the first familial occurrence in this entry, a heterozygous MPZ mutation confirmed in two generations. The variants already in the entry were all de novo in single patients. This supports autosomal dominant transmission, but the abstract does not specify the variant or give follow-up details.

*molecular_genetics*: In a family with congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy, Smit et al. (2008) confirmed a heterozygous mutation in the MPZ gene in 2 generations.

*inheritance* ⚠︎: Smit et al. (2008) described a family in which congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy with a heterozygous MPZ mutation occurred in 2 generations. This is consistent with autosomal dominant inheritance.

## [MEDIUM] Kochanski et al. (2004) — new_variants

Kochanski A, Drac H, Kabzińska D, Ryniewicz B, Rowińska-Marcińska K, Nowakowski A, Hausmanowa-Petrusewicz I. A novel MPZ gene mutation in congenital neuropathy with hypomyelination. Neurology 62: 2122-3, 2004. [PubMed: 15184631] *(read: abstract)*

**Why:** Kochanski et al. (2004) reported a new MPZ mutation, thr124-to-lys (T124K), that is not in the entry's variant table. They also described sural nerve biopsy findings the entry does not record: nonmyelinated and poorly myelinated axons, basal lamina onion bulbs and no myelin breakdown products.

*clinical_features* ⚠︎: Kochanski et al. (2004) described the clinical and morphologic features of a patient with congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy, a severe neuropathy with onset in early infancy. Sural nerve biopsy showed the characteristic findings of nonmyelinated and poorly myelinated axons with basal lamina onion bulbs and no myelin breakdown products.

*molecular_genetics*: In a patient with congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy, Kochanski et al. (2004) identified a novel thr124-to-lys (T124K) mutation in the MPZ gene.

## [MEDIUM] Nowakowski and Kochański (2004) — new_variants

Nowakowski A, Kochański A. Screening of the myelin protein zero gene in patients with Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease. Acta Biochim Pol 51: 273-80, 2004. [PubMed: 15094849] *(read: abstract)*

**Why:** Nowakowski and Kochański (2004) report a new MPZ variant, T124K, in a patient with congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy. The entry's variant table lists only Q215X and the complex deletion/insertion at nucleotides 550-552. Their E56K finding is in CMT2, a different disorder, so it is not relevant here.

*molecular_genetics*: Nowakowski and Kochański (2004) screened the coding and promoter sequences of the MPZ gene by SSCP and heteroduplex analysis in patients with CMT1, CMT2, and congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy (CHN). They identified a thr124-to-lys (T124K) substitution in MPZ in 1 patient with CHN.
