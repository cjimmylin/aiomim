### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that severe achondroplasia with developmental delay and acanthosis nigricans (SADDAN) is caused by mutation in the FGFR3 gene (134934) on chromosome 4p16.3.

### Description

SADDAN is a severe skeletal dysplasia that approaches the severity of thanatophoric dysplasia type I. It is caused by an activating mutation in FGFR3 (Tavormina et al., 1999). Bellus et al. (1999) described severe disturbances in endochondral bone growth that approach and overlap those of thanatophoric dysplasia type I. Some patients have femoral bowing with reverse tibial bowing. Other features include developmental delay and acanthosis nigricans beginning in early childhood, and some affected individuals have survived past infancy (Tavormina et al., 1999).

### Clinical Features

Tavormina et al. (1999) reported 4 unrelated individuals with a skeletal dysplasia that they named SADDAN. The skeletal dysplasia approached the severity of thanatophoric dysplasia type I. However, some patients survived past infancy without prolonged life-support measures. Three of the 4 individuals had severe neurological impairment and developmental delay. Three of the 4 developed extensive areas of acanthosis nigricans beginning in early childhood. ⚠︎

Bellus et al. (1999) noted that SADDAN involves severe disturbances in endochondral bone growth that approach and overlap those of thanatophoric dysplasia type I. Some patients have femoral bowing with reverse (posterior apex) tibial and fibular bowing.

### Molecular Genetics

In 4 unrelated individuals with SADDAN, Tavormina et al. (1999) identified an A1949T mutation in the FGFR3 gene. The mutation results in a lys650-to-met (K650M) substitution at a highly conserved codon in the activation loop of the kinase domain. In transient transfection studies, the K650M mutation caused a dramatic increase in constitutive receptor kinase activity.

Iwata et al. (2001) stated that, in vitro, FGFR3 carrying the SADDAN mutation is more activated than FGFR3 carrying the thanatophoric dysplasia type II mutation.
