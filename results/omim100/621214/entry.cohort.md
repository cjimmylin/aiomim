### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that adult-onset autosomal dominant leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy (ADLDWA) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the CST3 gene (604312) on chromosome 20p11.21.

### Description

Adult-onset autosomal dominant leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy is a leukodystrophy that begins in mid to older adulthood. Patients have recurrent episodes of hemiplegic migraine with transient unilateral focal deficits, together with slowly progressive motor symptoms and cognitive decline. Brain MRI shows characteristic involvement of the deep cerebral white matter and other specific sites, including the globus pallidus. Neuropathologic examination shows cystic degeneration of the white matter without angiopathy (Bergner et al., 2024).

### Clinical Features

Bergner et al. (2024) described 16 patients from 8 families with an adult-onset leukodystrophy. The patients had recurrent episodes of hemiplegic migraine with transient unilateral focal deficits, and slowly progressive motor symptoms and cognitive decline in mid to older adulthood. Some patients had acute deterioration with reduced consciousness, and some died early. The authors described MRI changes that they considered pathognomonic, at typical predilection sites. These changes involved the deep cerebral white matter, sparing a periventricular rim and a directly subcortical rim, as well as the middle blade of the corpus callosum, the posterior limb of the internal capsule, the middle cerebellar peduncles, the cerebral peduncles and, specifically, the globus pallidus. Histopathologic study of 2 autopsy cases showed no angiopathy. Instead, there was micro- to macrocystic degeneration of the white matter, with activation and degeneration or loss of astrocytes, and myelin loss with increased numbers of oligodendrocytes, some of them apoptotic. Bergner et al. (2024) noted that the clinical and radiologic features differed markedly from those of Icelandic cerebral amyloid angiopathy, which is caused by the leu68-to-asn substitution in CST3.

Zhang and Wu (2024) reported Chinese patients with adult-onset leukodystrophy caused by CST3 variants.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of adult-onset leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy in the families reported by Bergner et al. (2024) was consistent with autosomal dominant inheritance. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In 16 patients from 8 families with adult-onset leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy, Bergner et al. (2024) identified heterozygous mutations in the CST3 gene. Each family carried 1 of 4 different stop-gain or frameshift mutations.

Zhang and Wu (2024) reported CST3 variants as the cause of adult-onset leukodystrophy in Chinese patients.

### Pathogenesis

Bergner et al. (2024) compared the structures of the variant cystatin C proteins. They suggested that specific truncations of cystatin C cause abnormal function, possibly by making the protein more prone to aggregation, and stated that this effect remained to be confirmed.
