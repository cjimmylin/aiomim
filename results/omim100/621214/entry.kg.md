### Description

Adult-onset autosomal dominant leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy (ADLDWA) is caused by dominant stop-gain or frameshift mutations in the CST3 gene. Bergner et al. (2024) reported that its clinical and radiologic features differed markedly from Icelandic cerebral amyloid angiopathy, which is caused by the leu68-to-asn (L68N) substitution in CST3.

### Clinical Features

Bergner et al. (2024) reported 16 patients from 8 families with an adult-onset leukodystrophy. The patients had recurrent episodes of hemiplegic migraine with transient one-sided focal deficits. Motor symptoms and cognitive decline progressed slowly in mid to older adulthood. In some patients, a sudden clinical deterioration led to a long episode of reduced consciousness and even early death. Brain MRI showed characteristic changes at typical predilection sites. These involved the deep cerebral white matter, sparing a rim of periventricular and directly subcortical tissue. Other sites were the middle blade of the corpus callosum, the posterior limb of the internal capsule, the middle cerebellar peduncles, the cerebral peduncles and, specifically, the globus pallidus.

Bergner et al. (2024) studied 2 autopsy cases. Histopathology showed no angiopathy but did show micro- to macrocystic degeneration of the white matter. Astrocytes were activated at early stages and later showed severe degeneration and loss. Despite the loss of myelin, oligodendrocytes were increased in number, and some of them were apoptotic.

### Inheritance

Bergner et al. (2024) found that the transmission pattern of ADLDWA in the 8 families they studied was autosomal dominant. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In 16 patients from 8 families with ADLDWA, Bergner et al. (2024) identified 4 different heterozygous stop-gain or frameshift mutations in the CST3 gene. A structural comparison of the variants suggested that specific truncations of cystatin C cause abnormal protein function, possibly by making the protein more prone to aggregation. The authors noted that further studies were needed to confirm this effect. ⚠︎

Zhang and Wu (2024) reported Chinese patients with adult-onset leukodystrophy caused by mutations in the CST3 gene.
