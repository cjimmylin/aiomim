## LEUKODYSTROPHY, ADULT-ONSET, AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT, WITHOUT AMYLOID ANGIOPATHY; ADLDWA

### TEXT

This entry describes an adult-onset, autosomal dominant leukodystrophy in which, unlike related hereditary cerebral amyloid angiopathies, amyloid angiopathy is not present (Bergner et al., 2024). ⚠︎

### Description

Adult-onset autosomal dominant leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy (ADLDWA) is a neurodegenerative disorder of the cerebral white matter that begins in adulthood and is inherited as an autosomal dominant trait (Bergner et al., 2024). Brain imaging shows leukoencephalopathy. Bergner et al. (2024) found no amyloid angiopathy in the disorder, which sets it apart from hereditary cerebral amyloid angiopathies with white matter involvement. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

Bergner et al. (2024) reported that adult-onset leukodystrophy without amyloid angiopathy is transmitted as an autosomal dominant trait.
