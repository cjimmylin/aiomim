## BRAIN SMALL VESSEL DISEASE 4; BSVD4

### TEXT

This entry describes brain small vessel disease-4 (BSVD4), one of a genetically heterogeneous group of hereditary cerebral small vessel diseases. ⚠︎

### Description

Brain small vessel disease refers to hereditary disorders that affect the small arteries, arterioles, capillaries and venules of the brain. They typically cause white matter abnormalities (leukoencephalopathy), lacunar infarcts, microbleeds and/or intracerebral hemorrhage. Clinically, they may present with stroke, cognitive decline or other neurologic deficits. ✗

### Heterogeneity

Brain small vessel disease is genetically heterogeneous. BSVD1 is caused by heterozygous mutation in the COL4A1 gene on chromosome 13q34. BSVD2 is caused by heterozygous mutation in the COL4A2 gene on chromosome 13q34. BSVD3 is caused by biallelic mutation in the COLGALT1 gene. ✗
