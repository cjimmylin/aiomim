### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that cerebellar ataxia, impaired intellectual development, and dysequilibrium syndrome-4 (CAMRQ4) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the ATP8A2 gene (605870) on chromosome 13q12.13.

### Description

CAMRQ4 is an autosomal recessive neurodevelopmental disorder (Mohamadian et al., 2020). Its features include cerebellar ataxia and impaired intellectual development, and some affected individuals show quadrupedal locomotion (Onat et al., 2013). Severe hypotonia and optic atrophy have also been reported in patients with ATP8A2 mutations (Mohamadian et al., 2020), as have athetotic involuntary movements (Narishige et al., 2022).

### Clinical Features

Onat et al. (2013) described a consanguineous family from Turkey with 4 affected individuals who had cerebellar ataxia, impaired intellectual development, and dysequilibrium. The disorder in this family was associated with quadrupedal locomotion and cerebellar atrophy.

Mohamadian et al. (2020) summarized the patients with ATP8A2 mutations reported by Martin-Hernandez et al. (2016). These patients had encephalopathy, impaired intellectual development, hypotonia, and optic atrophy.

### Inheritance

Mohamadian et al. (2020) stated that inheritance is autosomal recessive in all of the CAMRQ phenotypes. The family reported by Onat et al. (2013) was consanguineous, and the disorder in that family was mapped by homozygosity mapping.

### Cytogenetics

Onat et al. (2013) noted an unrelated individual whose impaired intellectual development and severe hypotonia were associated with a de novo balanced t(10; 13) translocation that disrupted ATP8A2. Mohamadian et al. (2020) stated that this translocation interrupted the ATP8A2 coding sequence.

### Molecular Genetics

Onat et al. (2013) studied a consanguineous Turkish family with CAMRQ. Homozygosity mapping showed several shared homozygous regions, including chromosome 13q12. Targeted next-generation sequencing of an affected individual, followed by segregation analysis, identified a missense mutation in the ATP8A2 gene, I376M. The mutation segregated with the disorder in the family. ⚠︎

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 CEREBELLAR ATAXIA, IMPAIRED INTELLECTUAL DEVELOPMENT, AND DYSEQUILIBRIUM SYNDROME 4**  
ATP8A2, ILE376MET [p.Ile376Met]

Onat et al. (2013) studied a consanguineous Turkish family with CAMRQ4 and quadrupedal locomotion. Homozygosity mapping was followed by targeted sequencing, and in this family they identified an ile376-to-met (I376M) missense mutation in the ATP8A2 gene. The mutation segregated with the disorder.
