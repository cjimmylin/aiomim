### Description

The orofaciodigital syndromes are a clinically and genetically heterogeneous group of ciliopathies. They are defined by abnormalities of the oral cavity (such as lobulated or hamartomatous tongue, oral frenulae, and cleft lip and/or palate), the face, and the digits (such as polydactyly, syndactyly, and brachydactyly). Some patients also have brain malformations and involvement of other organs. OFD20 is one of the numbered subtypes. I do not remember its specific defining reports well enough to describe them. ✗

### Heterogeneity

Orofaciodigital syndrome is genetically heterogeneous. Most numbered subtypes are caused by mutations in genes that encode proteins of the primary cilium or the centrosome. OFD1, the X-linked dominant form, is caused by mutation in the OFD1 gene. Most other forms are autosomal recessive. ✗
