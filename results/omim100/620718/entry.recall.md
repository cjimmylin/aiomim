## OROFACIODIGITAL SYNDROME XX; OFD20

### TEXT

### Description

Bruel et al. (2023) described the oral-facial-digital syndromes (OFDS) as a group of clinically and genetically heterogeneous disorders. They are characterized by defects in the development of the face and oral cavity, along with digit anomalies. They are ciliopathies: pathogenic variants in genes encoding ciliary proteins cause OFDS through deleterious structural or functional impacts on primary cilia (Bruel et al., 2023). Bruel et al. (2023) described a novel form of OFDS accompanied by cardiac, cerebral, skeletal, and anorectal defects.

### Heterogeneity

Orofaciodigital syndrome is genetically heterogeneous. Bruel et al. (2023) noted that pathogenic variants in over 20 genes encoding ciliary proteins had been found to cause OFDS through deleterious structural or functional impacts on primary cilia.
