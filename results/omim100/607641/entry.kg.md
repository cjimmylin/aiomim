### Description

Distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-14 is a lower motor neuron disease. Puls et al. (2003) linked it to chromosome 2p13. They found a mutation in the gene encoding the largest subunit of dynactin, a protein that carries material along axons. The mutation was predicted to affect dynactin's microtubule-binding domain. ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

In a lower motor neuron disease linked to chromosome 2p13, Puls et al. (2003) analyzed the gene encoding the largest subunit of the axonal transport protein dynactin (DCTN1). They found a single base-pair change that caused an amino acid substitution. The substitution was predicted to distort the folding of dynactin's microtubule-binding domain. In binding assays, the mutant protein bound less to microtubules.

### Pathogenesis

In binding assays by Puls et al. (2003), mutant dynactin bound less to microtubules. The authors concluded that faulty dynactin-mediated transport can lead to human motor neuron disease.
