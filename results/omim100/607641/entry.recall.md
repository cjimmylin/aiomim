### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal dominant distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-14 (HMND14) is caused by mutation in the dynactin gene (DCTN1; 601143) on chromosome 2p13.1. ⚠︎

### Description

Autosomal dominant distal hereditary motor neuronopathy-14 is a lower motor neuron disease. Puls et al. (2003) showed that it is caused by mutation in the gene encoding the largest subunit of the axonal transport protein dynactin. ⚠︎

### Mapping

Puls et al. (2003) studied a lower motor neuron disease and mapped the disease locus to chromosome 2p13 by linkage analysis. The linked interval contained the DCTN1 gene.

### Molecular Genetics

Puls et al. (2003) analysed the DCTN1 gene, which lies in the 2p13 linkage interval and encodes the largest subunit of dynactin. In patients with lower motor neuron disease, they found a single base-pair change resulting in an amino acid substitution. The substitution was predicted to distort the folding of dynactin's microtubule-binding domain. In binding assays, the mutant protein bound microtubules less well than normal protein. Puls et al. (2003) concluded that dysfunction of dynactin-mediated transport can lead to human motor neuron disease.
