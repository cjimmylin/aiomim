## NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER WITH HYPOTONIA, POOR GROWTH, DYSMORPHIC FACIES, AND AGAMMAGLOBULINEMIA; NEDHGFA

### TEXT

This entry describes a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by hypotonia, poor growth, dysmorphic facial features, and agammaglobulinemia (NEDHGFA), as reported by Weis et al. (2024).

### Description

NEDHGFA is a disorder of early childhood (Weis et al., 2024). Its cardinal features are global developmental delay, hypotonia, poor growth, dysmorphic facial features, and agammaglobulinemia. The agammaglobulinemia occurs with a B-cell deficiency that predisposes to recurrent infections (Weis et al., 2024).
