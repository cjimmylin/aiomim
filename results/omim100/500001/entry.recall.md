### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Leber optic atrophy and dystonia (LDYT) is caused by mutation in the mitochondrial MTND6 gene (516006) or the mitochondrial MTND4 gene (516003).

### Description

Leber optic atrophy and dystonia is a maternally inherited mitochondrial disorder (Jun et al., 1994). Families with Leber hereditary optic neuropathy (LHON) and/or dystonia have been reported. In these families the generalized dystonia was attributed to striatal degeneration (Watanabe et al., 2006). The disorder is caused by mitochondrial DNA mutations in complex I (NADH dehydrogenase) subunit genes (De Vries et al., 1996). A mutation in the MTND6 gene has been suggested as the disease-causing mutation in several families (Watanabe et al., 2006). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Novotny et al. (1986) studied a large kindred in which 8 members had the neuroretinopathy of Leber disease and 14 had a progressive, generalized dystonia attributed to striatal degeneration. One member had both disorders.

Marsden et al. (1986) reported familial dystonia with visual failure. Affected individuals had bilateral striatal lucencies on CT scan, especially involving the putamen.

### Inheritance

In the kindred reported by Novotny et al. (1986), the mode of inheritance was compatible with maternal transmission, and the authors suggested that the disorder may be a mitochondrial disease. Jun et al. (1994) found an mtDNA mutation in 6 maternal relatives in 3 generations of a family, 1 of whom was heteroplasmic. This confirmed transmission through the maternal line.

### Molecular Genetics

Jun et al. (1994) studied a 5-generation Hispanic family with maternally transmitted LHON and/or early-onset dystonia. In the MTND6 gene they found a G-to-A transition at nucleotide 14459, which changes alanine to valine at residue 72 (A72V). The mutation segregated through the maternal line and was associated with both LHON and dystonia.

De Vries et al. (1996) studied a large Dutch family with LHON associated with hereditary spastic dystonia. Affected members carried the 11696 mutation in the MTND4 gene together with a mutation at nucleotide 14596 in the MTND6 gene. ⚠︎
