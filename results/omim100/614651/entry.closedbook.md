### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that primary coenzyme Q10 deficiency-2 (COQ10D2) is caused by homozygous mutation in the PDSS1 gene (607429) on chromosome 10p12.

### Description

Primary coenzyme Q10 deficiency-2 is an autosomal recessive mitochondrial disorder caused by a defect in the first step of the coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone) biosynthetic pathway. PDSS1 encodes a subunit of the prenyldiphosphate synthase that makes the polyprenyl side chain of coenzyme Q10. The disorder was first described in sibs who had early-onset deafness, encephaloneuropathy, obesity, livedo reticularis, and heart valve disease. ✗

### Clinical Features

Mollet et al. (2007) reported 2 sibs born to consanguineous parents. They had early-onset sensorineural deafness, encephaloneuropathy, obesity, livedo reticularis, and cardiac valvulopathy. Their cultured skin fibroblasts showed low coenzyme Q10 content and reduced activity of the respiratory chain complexes that depend on quinone. ✗

### Biochemical Features

In the patients' fibroblasts, Mollet et al. (2007) found coenzyme Q10 deficiency and reduced quinone-dependent respiratory chain activities (complexes I+III and II+III). Adding coenzyme Q10 to the culture medium restored these activities. ✗

### Inheritance

COQ10D2 shows autosomal recessive inheritance. Mollet et al. (2007) reported affected sibs from a consanguineous family who were homozygous for a PDSS1 mutation. ✗

### Molecular Genetics

In 2 sibs with primary coenzyme Q10 deficiency born to consanguineous parents, Mollet et al. (2007) found a homozygous missense mutation in the PDSS1 gene (D308E). In functional studies, the wildtype human gene complemented a yeast mutant lacking the orthologous gene (coq1), but the mutant gene did so less effectively. ✗

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 COENZYME Q10 DEFICIENCY, PRIMARY, 2**  
PDSS1, ASP308GLU [p.Asp308Glu]

In 2 sibs with primary coenzyme Q10 deficiency-2 born to consanguineous parents, Mollet et al. (2007) found a homozygous asp308-to-glu (D308E) substitution in the PDSS1 gene. The patients had early-onset deafness, encephaloneuropathy, obesity, livedo reticularis, and valvulopathy. In yeast complementation studies, the mutant was less effective than wildtype at rescuing a coq1-deficient strain. ✗
