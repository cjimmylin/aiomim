A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that atrial standstill-1 (ATRST1) can be caused by heterozygous mutation in the SCN5A gene (600163) on chromosome 3p22, occurring together with variants in the connexin-40 gene GJA5 (121013) on chromosome 1q21, consistent with digenic inheritance. ⚠︎

### Description

Atrial standstill is a rare arrhythmia in which the atria show no electrical or mechanical activity. On the electrocardiogram there are no P waves, and the rhythm is a slow junctional escape rhythm. Atrial stimulation cannot capture the atria. The condition can lead to thromboembolism and usually needs pacemaker implantation. In familial cases, the disorder has been linked to mutation in the cardiac sodium channel gene SCN5A occurring together with variants in the atrial gap junction gene GJA5 (connexin-40). ✗

### Clinical Features

Groenewegen et al. (2003) studied a family with familial atrial standstill. Affected members had no P waves, bradycardia with junctional escape rhythm, and atria that did not respond to electrical stimulation. ✗

Makita et al. (2005) described a Japanese family with congenital atrial standstill. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

The findings of Groenewegen et al. (2003) suggested digenic inheritance of familial atrial standstill. Affected members carried both an SCN5A mutation and a rare GJA5 (connexin-40) genotype, whereas family members with the SCN5A mutation alone were unaffected.

### Heterogeneity

Atrial standstill is genetically heterogeneous. An autosomal recessive form, atrial standstill-2 (ATRST2), is caused by mutation in the NPPA gene. ✗

### Molecular Genetics

In a family with atrial standstill, Groenewegen et al. (2003) identified a heterozygous asp1275-to-asn (D1275N) mutation in the SCN5A gene. The mutation segregated with the disease together with a rare genotype of 2 linked polymorphisms in the regulatory region of the connexin-40 gene (GJA5). The authors proposed that the combination of reduced sodium channel function and altered connexin-40 expression caused the loss of atrial excitability. In their functional studies, the D1275N channel showed only modest changes in gating properties. ⚠︎

In a Japanese family with congenital atrial standstill, Makita et al. (2005) found a novel SCN5A mutation that was inherited together with connexin-40 polymorphisms. This supported the model of SCN5A and GJA5 coinheritance proposed by Groenewegen et al. (2003). ⚠︎
