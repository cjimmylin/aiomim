### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2A1 (CMT2A1) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the KIF1B gene (605995) on chromosome 1p36. ⚠︎

### Description

Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2A1 is an autosomal dominant axonal (non-demyelinating) peripheral neuropathy marked by slowly progressive distal muscle weakness and atrophy, more severe in the lower limbs, with distal sensory loss and reduced or absent tendon reflexes. Nerve conduction velocities are normal or only mildly reduced. The axonal forms of CMT are grouped as CMT2. CMT2A is genetically heterogeneous: CMT2A1 is caused by mutation in KIF1B, and the more common CMT2A2 is caused by mutation in MFN2. ✗

### Mapping

Ben Othmane et al. (1993) used linkage analysis in a large family with autosomal dominant axonal CMT to map the CMT2A locus to the distal short arm of chromosome 1 (1p35-p36). ✗

Saito et al. (1997) reported linkage of axonal CMT to the same region of chromosome 1p36 in a Japanese family. ✗

### Heterogeneity

Bissar-Tadmouri et al. (2004) found no KIF1B mutation in a large Turkish family with CMT2A linked to 1p36, which pointed to a second gene in the region. Zuchner et al. (2004) then found mutations in the MFN2 gene, which also maps to 1p36, in several families with CMT2A. CMT2A caused by MFN2 mutation is designated CMT2A2, and KIF1B mutations seem to be a rare cause of CMT2A. ✗

### Molecular Genetics

In affected members of a Japanese family with CMT2A linked to 1p36, Zhao et al. (2001) found a heterozygous gln98-to-leu (Q98L) mutation in the KIF1B gene, which encodes a kinesin motor protein. The mutation lies in the conserved ATP-binding consensus motif of the motor domain, and the mutant protein had reduced motor activity. KIF1B-beta transports synaptic vesicle precursors along axons. ✗

### Animal Model

Zhao et al. (2001) found that homozygous Kif1b knockout mice died soon after birth, with defects in the nervous system. Heterozygous Kif1b +/- mice had defective transport of synaptic vesicle precursors and developed a progressive peripheral neuropathy resembling human CMT2A, with muscle weakness. ⚠︎
