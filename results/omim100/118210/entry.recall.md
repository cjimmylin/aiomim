### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2A1 (CMT2A1) is caused by mutation in the KIF1B gene (605995) on chromosome 1p36.

### Description

Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2A1 is an autosomal dominant axonal (non-demyelinating) peripheral neuropathy that causes progressive muscle weakness (Xu et al., 2018). CMT is divided into 2 main types: demyelinating CMT1, with nerve conduction velocities below 38 m/s, and axonal CMT2, with conduction velocities above 38 m/s, which are in the normal range (Xu et al., 2018). CMT2A is genetically heterogeneous. CMT2A1 is caused by mutation in the KIF1B gene (Zhao et al., 2001), and CMT2A2, the most common known form, is caused by mutation in the MFN2 gene (Xu et al., 2018). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Saito et al. (1997) reported that in their CMT2 patients, tendon reflexes were preserved in the upper extremities but reduced or absent in the lower extremities.

Xu et al. (2018) described a patient with a progressive, symmetric, length-dependent, sensorimotor, predominantly axonal polyneuropathy.

### Inheritance

Xu et al. (2018) reported autosomal dominant inheritance of CMT2A1.

### Mapping

Ben Othmane et al. (1993) carried out pedigree linkage analysis in 6 large autosomal dominant CMT2 families. Three of the families showed linkage, and the authors mapped the CMT2A locus to the distal region of the short arm of chromosome 1.

Saito et al. (1997) carried out linkage analysis in 2 Japanese CMT2 families using markers flanking the CMT2A, CMT2B, and CMT2D loci. They obtained a highest cumulative multipoint lod score of 3.69 at D1S244 on chromosome 1p35-p36. ⚠︎

### Heterogeneity

Xu et al. (2018) noted that Zuchner et al. (2004) had found MFN2 point mutations in several CMT2A pedigrees. The MFN2 gene lies in the same genomic interval as KIF1B. CMT2A caused by MFN2 mutation is designated CMT2A2 and is the most common known axonal form. Although a KIF1B mutation was the first to be identified in a pedigree with CMT2A, only a few pedigrees with KIF1B-beta mutations causing CMT2A1 have been reported (Xu et al., 2018). ⚠︎

### Molecular Genetics

Zhao et al. (2001) showed that patients with CMT2A carried a loss-of-function mutation in the motor domain of the KIF1B gene, which encodes a kinesin superfamily motor protein.

### Animal Model

Zhao et al. (2001) found that Kif1b knockout mice died at birth from nervous system defects. Heterozygous Kif1b mice had a defect in transporting synaptic vesicle precursors and suffered from progressive muscle weakness similar to human neuropathies.
