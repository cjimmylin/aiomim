### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that familial hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis-4 (FHL4) is caused by homozygous mutation in the gene encoding syntaxin-11 (STX11; 605014) on chromosome 6q24.2.

### Description

Familial hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis (FHL) is a rare autosomal recessive disorder (zur Stadt et al., 2005) that affects young children (Zur Stadt et al., 2006). Müller et al. (2014) noted that the diagnostic criteria include fever, splenomegaly, bicytopenia, hypertriglyceridemia and/or hypofibrinogenemia, and low or absent natural killer (NK) cell activity. FHL4 is the form caused by deficiency of syntaxin-11 (zur Stadt et al., 2005). Syntaxin-11 is involved in cytotoxic lymphocyte exocytosis (Müller et al., 2014). ⚠︎

### Mapping

In a large consanguineous Kurdish kindred with FHL, zur Stadt et al. (2005) performed genome-wide homozygosity mapping and found linkage to a 10-cM region on chromosome 6q24 between D6S1569 and D6S960, which defined a new FHL locus.

### Heterogeneity

Familial hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis is genetically heterogeneous. zur Stadt et al. (2005) noted that FHL2 and FHL3 are caused by mutations in the perforin gene (PRF1) and in UNC13D, respectively. Müller et al. (2014) noted that FHL5 is associated with mutations in Munc18-2, which is encoded by STXBP2.

### Molecular Genetics

zur Stadt et al. (2005) screened positional candidate genes in the 6q24 region in a large consanguineous Kurdish kindred with FHL. They identified a homozygous 5-bp deletion in the STX11 gene. They found homozygous STX11 mutations in five consanguineous Turkish/Kurdish FHL kindreds. In one family the mutation was a homozygous 19.2-kb genomic deletion spanning the entire coding region of STX11 (exon 2).

Rudd et al. (2006) used direct sequencing to screen STX11 in 28 FHL families that did not have perforin mutations. They found STX11 mutations affecting 6 of 34 children in 4 of the 28 unrelated PRF1-negative families. Rudd et al. (2006) noted a milder phenotype in these patients: some had long periods (1 year or more) in remission without specific treatment, which is very uncommon in this disease.

### Pathogenesis

Müller et al. (2014) noted that syntaxin-11 deficiency abolishes degranulation by both cytotoxic T cells and NK cells.

### Animal Model

Müller et al. (2014) noted that Stx11-deficient mice, like patients with STX11 nonsense mutations, typically have less severe disease than other FHL subtypes.
