### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Pallister-Hall-like syndrome (PHLS) is caused by biallelic mutation in the SMO gene (601500) on chromosome 7q32.1. ⚠︎

### Description

Pallister-Hall-like syndrome is a developmental disorder with a wide phenotypic spectrum (Le et al., 2020). Features include polydactyly, and a hypothalamic hamartoma has been seen in some patients (Rubino et al., 2019). Le et al. (2020) also reported shortening of the long bones and heart malformations. The disorder results from defective Hedgehog signaling (Le et al., 2020).

### Clinical Features

Encha-Razavi et al. (1992) reported cases whose features showed similarities to those of Pallister-Hall syndrome.

### Inheritance

Le et al. (2020) found biallelic loss-of-function SMO variants in 7 individuals from 5 independent families. This finding is consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance.

### Molecular Genetics

Rubino et al. (2019) described a familial syndrome of hypothalamic hamartomas and polydactyly caused by germline mutations in the SMO gene.

Le et al. (2020) identified biallelic loss-of-function variants in the SMO gene in 7 individuals from 5 independent families with a wide phenotypic spectrum of developmental anomalies. In functional studies, cells derived from affected individuals showed severely altered Hedgehog signal transduction.
