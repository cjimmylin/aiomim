### Description

Developmental delay with variable intellectual disability and dysmorphic facies (DIDDF) is a neurodevelopmental disorder (Verberne et al., 2021). Verberne et al. (2022) described it as characterized by developmental delay and by cognitive impairment that ranges from borderline intellectual functioning to severe intellectual disability. Cadieux-Dion et al. (2022) listed dysmorphic features among its characteristic features.
