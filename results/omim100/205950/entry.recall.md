### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that pyridoxine-refractory sideroblastic anemia-2 (SIDBA2) is caused by mutation in the SLC25A38 gene (610819) on chromosome 3p22.1. ⚠︎

### Description

Sideroblastic anemia-2 is an autosomal recessive, nonsyndromic congenital sideroblastic anemia (Guernsey et al., 2009). The morphologic hallmark of the sideroblastic anemias is ringed sideroblasts. These are bone marrow erythroid precursors containing pathologic iron deposits within mitochondria (Guernsey et al., 2009). Manabe et al. (1982) reported onset in infancy and no response to pyridoxine. Jardine et al. (1994) described severe, transfusion-dependent anemia. ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Guernsey et al. (2009) studied families with nonsyndromic congenital sideroblastic anemia that was inherited in an autosomal recessive pattern. They noted that the morphologic hallmark of the sideroblastic anemias is ringed sideroblasts in the bone marrow. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

Guernsey et al. (2009) defined an autosomal recessive form of nonsyndromic congenital sideroblastic anemia associated with mutations in the SLC25A38 gene.

### Heterogeneity

Guernsey et al. (2009) noted that the sideroblastic anemias are a heterogeneous group of congenital and acquired hematologic disorders. Jardine et al. (1994) noted that ALAS2 mutations had been reported in patients with X-linked sideroblastic anemia. SIDBA2 is an autosomal recessive form of congenital sideroblastic anemia associated with SLC25A38 mutation (Guernsey et al., 2009).

### Molecular Genetics

Guernsey et al. (2009) used positional cloning to define a previously unknown form of autosomal recessive nonsyndromic congenital sideroblastic anemia. They found that it was associated with mutations in the SLC25A38 gene, which encodes an erythroid-specific mitochondrial carrier family protein.

### Clinical Management

Manabe et al. (1982) reported a patient with congenital sideroblastic anemia who did not respond to pyridoxine therapy. The patients reported by Jardine et al. (1994) had transfusion-dependent, pyridoxine-refractory sideroblastic anemia from birth.
