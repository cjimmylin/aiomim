### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene (301057) on chromosome Xp21.1.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 is a form of male infertility characterized by asthenoteratozoospermia. Sperm motility is reduced and the sperm flagella show abnormal morphology, placing the disorder within the spectrum of multiple morphologic abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al. (2021)).

### Inheritance

Liu et al. (2021) established that SPGFX3 is inherited in an X-linked pattern: affected men carry hemizygous mutations in CFAP47, a gene on the X chromosome.

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) performed whole-exome sequencing in infertile men with asthenoteratozoospermia and identified hemizygous mutations in the CFAP47 gene. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence assays showed obviously reduced levels of CFAP47 in spermatozoa from all three men carrying deleterious missense variants in CFAP47. Liu et al. (2021) linked loss of CFAP47 to defective sperm flagellar structure and reduced sperm motility.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) found that adult male mice with Cfap47 mutations were sterile and had reduced sperm motility, a phenotype resembling the asthenoteratozoospermia seen in men with CFAP47 mutations.
