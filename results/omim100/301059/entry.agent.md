### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene on chromosome Xp21.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is a form of male infertility characterized by asthenoteratozoospermia with multiple morphological abnormalities of the flagella (MMAF) (Liu et al., 2021). Affected men show reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar morphology (Liu et al., 2021).

### Clinical Features

Liu et al. (2021) studied 3 unrelated Chinese men with asthenoteratozoospermia characterized by multiple morphological abnormalities of the flagella (MMAF), as well as a man from an Australian cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia. All of the men displayed typical MMAF phenotypes. Liu et al. (2021) suggested that the prognosis with intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI) was good.

Liao et al. (2022) screened 7 men with MMAF and identified 1 man with mutations in the CFAP47 gene. In spermatozoa from this man, the expression of CFAP47 was dramatically reduced, and expression of WDR87 was also significantly decreased, with weak signals detected adjacent to the sperm nuclei.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of SPGFX3 is X-linked, as established by Liu et al. (2021), who found hemizygous variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene in affected men.

### Molecular Genetics

By whole-exome sequencing in 3 unrelated Chinese men with MMAF, Liu et al. (2021) identified hemizygous missense variants in the X-linked CFAP47 gene. The 3 variants were absent from human control population genome databases and were predicted to be deleterious by multiple bioinformatic tools. Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed obviously reduced levels of CFAP47 in spermatozoa from all 3 men. Analysis of whole-exome sequencing data from an additional cohort of men with severe asthenoteratozoospermia originating from Australia identified a man with a hemizygous Xp21.1 deletion removing the entire CFAP47 gene.

In 1 of 7 men with MMAF, Liao et al. (2022) identified 2 mutations in the CFAP47 gene, c.706G-A (V236M) and c.1337C-T (T446M). Immunoblotting and immunofluorescence showed dramatically reduced CFAP47 in spermatozoa from this man. Liao et al. (2022) also found that CFAP47 interacted with WDR87 in the flagellar midpiece of spermatozoa and concluded that CFAP47 was necessary for WDR87 transport during sperm flagellar biogenesis.

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) generated a Cfap47-mutated mouse model. Adult male mice were sterile and showed reduced sperm motility and abnormal flagellar morphology and movement. Fertility could be rescued by intracytoplasmic sperm injection.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 SPERMATOGENIC FAILURE, X-LINKED, 3**  
CFAP47, VAL236MET [c.706G>A; p.Val236Met]

In a man with multiple morphological abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF), Liao et al. (2022) identified a c.706G-A transition in the CFAP47 gene, resulting in a val236-to-met (V236M) substitution, together with a second CFAP47 mutation (T446M). CFAP47 was dramatically reduced in spermatozoa from this man.
