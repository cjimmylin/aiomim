### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 (SPGFX3) is caused by hemizygous mutation in the CFAP47 gene on chromosome Xp21.

### Description

X-linked spermatogenic failure-3 is a form of male infertility marked by asthenoteratozoospermia. Sperm motility is reduced and the sperm flagella show abnormal shapes, which places the disorder within the spectrum of multiple morphologic abnormalities of the sperm flagella (MMAF).

### Inheritance

SPGFX3 is inherited in an X-linked pattern. Affected men carry hemizygous mutations in CFAP47, an X-chromosome gene (Liu et al., 2021).

### Molecular Genetics

Liu et al. (2021) studied infertile men with asthenoteratozoospermia using whole-exome sequencing and found hemizygous mutations in the CFAP47 gene. In the sperm of the affected men, CFAP47 protein was reduced or absent. The authors linked the loss of CFAP47 to defective flagellar structure and reduced sperm motility. ⚠︎

### Animal Model

Liu et al. (2021) reported that male mice with Cfap47 mutations had reduced sperm motility and lower fertility, a phenotype resembling the asthenoteratozoospermia seen in the affected men. ⚠︎
