## NEURODEVELOPMENTAL DISORDER WITH HYPOTONIA, LANGUAGE DELAY, AND SKELETAL DEFECTS WITH OR WITHOUT SEIZURES; NEDHLSS

### TEXT

This entry describes a neurodevelopmental disorder in which Rodan et al. (2021) reported developmental delay, hypotonia, language delay, and orthopedic abnormalities, with epilepsy in some patients. The audited evidence does not establish a causal gene.

### Description

Rodan et al. (2021) described developmental delay as a core feature of the disorder: all 14 individuals with non-truncating variants had a history of developmental delay or intellectual disability. Language was especially affected: 10 of 11 individuals with truncating variants had delayed expressive language, and 9 of those with non-truncating variants were nonverbal. Among individuals with non-truncating variants, 12 of 14 were hypotonic and 12 of 14 had orthopedic abnormalities, including clubfoot, hip dysplasia, kyphoscoliosis, and syndactyly. Some patients had seizures. Epilepsy was present in 10 of 14 individuals with non-truncating variants, with onset ranging from 2 months to 8 years, and in 1 of 11 individuals with truncating variants. ⚠︎
