### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that axonal Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2EE (CMT2EE) is caused by homozygous mutation in the MPV17 gene. ⚠︎

### Description

Axonal Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2EE (CMT2EE) is an autosomal recessive axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy with juvenile or adult onset. Affected individuals have distal muscle weakness and atrophy, mainly in the lower limbs, along with distal sensory loss. They do not have the liver or brain involvement seen in Navajo neurohepatopathy, which is also caused by MPV17 mutations (Choi et al., 2015; Baumann et al., 2019). ⚠︎

### Clinical Features

Choi et al. (2015) reported 2 unrelated Korean patients, each born to healthy nonconsanguineous parents, with axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy. Patient 1, a 34-year-old woman, first noticed weakness of the distal lower limbs at 9 years of age and began walking with short leg braces at 12 years. On examination at 34 years, she had bilateral distal muscle weakness and atrophy, mainly in the lower limbs. She had severe atrophy of the intrinsic hand, foot, and calf muscles, flexion deformities of the interphalangeal joints, ankle joint deformity, and scoliosis. Vibration and position senses were more severely affected than pain and touch, and knee and ankle jerks were absent. Patient 2, a 22-year-old man, first had gait and balance problems at age 13 and began walking with short leg braces at 20 years. He had bilateral distal weakness and atrophy, flat feet, absent deep tendon reflexes in all extremities, and the same predominant loss of vibration and position sense. Neither patient had other features of Navajo neurohepatopathy, such as hepatomegaly, cognitive impairment, ophthalmoplegia, or corneal scarring. Serum lactate was mildly elevated in both patients (2.1 and 1.9 mmol/L).

Electrophysiologic studies in the patients of Choi et al. (2015) showed loss of sural sensory nerve action potentials early in the disease. Median and ulnar nerves had reduced motor conduction velocities and CMAPs, peroneal and tibial CMAPs could not be elicited, and no sensory responses were recordable. Visual and brainstem auditory evoked potentials were normal. Muscle MRI of the lower leg showed severe atrophy and fatty replacement mainly in the soleus muscles, with relative sparing of the tibialis posterior and lateral gastrocnemius; the authors considered this pattern consistent with length-dependent axonal degeneration. Brain MRI was normal in both patients. In patient 1, sural nerve biopsy at 34 years showed complete loss of large and medium-sized myelinated fibers, with no demyelination, remyelination, or onion bulbs. In patient 2, vastus lateralis muscle biopsy at 22 years showed a few ragged-red fibers and subsarcolemmal accumulation of enlarged mitochondria.

Baumann et al. (2019) described 5 patients from 2 unrelated families with sensorimotor axonal neuropathy without hepatocerebral involvement. According to the title of the report, onset was juvenile or adult.

Blakely et al. (2012) reported a 21-year-old Pakistani man, born to nonconsanguineous parents, with a phenotype the authors considered similar to the classic form of Navajo neurohepatopathy. He had walking difficulties from age 14 years that progressed to bilateral foot drop, together with distal sensory loss, distal muscle wasting, and absent tendon reflexes. Nerve conduction studies showed an axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy. Unlike the patients described by Choi et al. (2015) and Baumann et al. (2019), he also had leukoencephalopathy on brain MRI, raised CSF protein and lactate, mild hepatomegaly, and patchy hepatic steatosis with early bridging fibrosis on liver biopsy. His muscle biopsy showed ragged-red fibers, about 20% COX-deficient fibers, and multiple mtDNA deletions with normal mtDNA copy number. His sister died of a progressive hepatitis that began at age 9 years. ⚠︎

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of CMT2EE in the families reported by Choi et al. (2015) and Baumann et al. (2019) was consistent with autosomal recessive inheritance.

### Molecular Genetics

Choi et al. (2015) performed whole-exome sequencing on 3 members of each of 2 unrelated Korean families with axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy. In the affected individual of each family they found the same homozygous c.122G-A transition in the MPV17 gene, resulting in an arg41-to-gln (R41Q) substitution. Sanger sequencing confirmed the mutation, and in both families a mutant allele was putatively inherited from each parent. The mutation was absent from 300 controls and 302 in-house exomes and was not in dbSNP142 or the 1000 Genomes Project database. It was present at very low frequency in the Exome Sequencing Project (0.00008) and ExAC (0.00002471) databases. In NSC34 mouse motor neuronal cells, knockdown of Mpv17 reduced cell proliferation and viability under oxidative stress, and expression of the R41Q mutant protein significantly inhibited cell proliferation. The authors suggested that both loss of normal MPV17 function and harmful effects of the mutant protein might affect neuronal function.

In 5 patients from 2 unrelated families with axonal sensorimotor neuropathy, Baumann et al. (2019) identified homozygous variants in the MPV17 gene. Affected members of the first family carried the c.122G-A (R41Q) mutation. Affected members of the second family carried a novel c.376-9T-G near-splice mutation, which was shown to cause an in-frame deletion of 11 amino acids.

### ALLELIC VARIANTS

**.0001 CHARCOT-MARIE-TOOTH DISEASE, AXONAL, TYPE 2EE**  
MPV17, ARG41GLN [c.122G>A; p.R41Q; p.Arg41Gln]

In 2 unrelated Korean patients with axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy without liver or brain involvement, Choi et al. (2015) identified a homozygous c.122G-A transition in the MPV17 gene, resulting in an arg41-to-gln (R41Q) substitution at a residue conserved across vertebrate species. The mutation was found by whole-exome sequencing and confirmed by Sanger sequencing. It was absent from 300 controls and was present at very low frequency in the ESP and ExAC databases. Expression of the mutant protein in NSC34 mouse motor neuronal cells significantly inhibited cell proliferation.

Baumann et al. (2019) found the c.122G-A (R41Q) mutation in the homozygous state in affected members of 1 of 2 families with sensorimotor axonal neuropathy without hepatocerebral involvement.

**.0002 CHARCOT-MARIE-TOOTH DISEASE, AXONAL, TYPE 2EE**  
MPV17, IVS, T-G, -9 [c.376-9T>G]

In affected members of a family with sensorimotor axonal neuropathy without hepatocerebral involvement, Baumann et al. (2019) identified a homozygous c.376-9T-G near-splice mutation in the MPV17 gene, which was shown to cause an in-frame deletion of 11 amino acids.
