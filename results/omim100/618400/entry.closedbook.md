### TEXT

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive axonal Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2EE (CMT2EE) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the MPV17 gene (137960) on chromosome 2p23. ⚠︎

### Description

Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 2EE (CMT2EE) is an autosomal recessive axonal sensorimotor peripheral neuropathy. It begins in childhood or later, with distal muscle weakness and atrophy, mainly in the lower limbs, along with distal sensory loss. Some patients have features suggesting mitochondrial dysfunction. Other mutations in MPV17 cause a severe infantile hepatocerebral mitochondrial DNA depletion syndrome, which includes Navajo neurohepatopathy.

### Inheritance

The transmission pattern of CMT2EE is autosomal recessive.

### Molecular Genetics

Baumann et al. (2019) found homozygous or compound heterozygous mutations in the MPV17 gene in patients with juvenile- or adult-onset axonal sensorimotor polyneuropathy. The mutations included a recurrent arg41-to-gln (R41Q) substitution. ✗
